# Go-To-Market — Questify

> How the first organizers are reached, activated and retained. Draft 2026-10-17 (Stage 3).
> No pilot event is scheduled yet (Q-022).

> **Stage 5 finding.** The user chose "all of it" when asked for the one reason an organizer picks
> Questify. A pitch that lists everything is harder to win with. Claude's suggested lead, if one is
> needed: sponsor value (real numbers for sponsors) together with prizes everyone can win.

## Beachhead audience

**Organizers in the founder's own network, running festivals and exhibitions.** They feel the
problem most (a large audience, sponsors to satisfy, prizes to give) and can be reached directly.
Their events are also visible, which makes the next sale easier.

## Acquisition channels

1. **Direct outreach and demos.** The founder shows organizers their own event running in
   rehearsal mode on real phones (ADR-080). *Working looks like:* three organizers reach a
   rehearsal and one asks for activation.
2. **Event agencies as resellers.** One agency runs many events; managed events (ADR-077) let
   Questify build in the client's account and hand it over. *Working looks like:* one agency runs
   two or more events through Questify.

WhatsApp and LinkedIn presence, and the self-serve landing page, are not focus channels at
launch.

## Launch plan

1. Finish the build and pass store review; run Google's 14-day closed test with 12+ testers
   (ADR-010) in parallel.
2. Show the rehearsal demo to the first organizers; agree price and add-ons outside the platform.
3. Activate the first event; the team supports it through the WhatsApp hotline (ADR-079).
4. Measure activation and install-to-first-coin (METRICS.md).
5. Collect the organizer's feedback, hand over the post-event report (F-208), and ask for a
   rebooking or an introduction.

## Activation moment

A player scans the event's join QR and completes a first quest within 3 minutes of the first scan (install included),
seeing the first coin fly to their balance. This is the activation metric in METRICS.md.

## First retention and referral loop

- **Players:** the Questify profile, passport and achievements (ADR-044) give a reason to join
  the next event; event recaps are shareable (F-141).
- **Organizers:** the post-event report and a dashboard they can duplicate for next year
  (F-190); agencies bring further clients.
