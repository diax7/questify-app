read as the Quests tab, and drops pins whose quest it does not list.

## Care points

- A quest has one pin; two writes for one quest at once: one wins, the other gets `quest_pinned` (a test fires both).
- "You are here" is the quest of the player's latest `qr`, `my_qr`, `nfc` or `location` completion, if it has a pin; never stored; no
  phone position is read anywhere (a test reads the app files).
- A hidden quest, a quest in a hidden category the player has not revealed, and a quest of a switched-off type never appear on a map.
- The board is never mirrored for Arabic; the pins and the page text follow the language.
