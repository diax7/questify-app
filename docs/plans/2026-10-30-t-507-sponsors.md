## Split (recorded in TASKS)

- **T-507 (this task):** sponsor profiles (name in one language at least, tier, booth), tags on quests, categories and rewards, the
  dashboard's Sponsors page, "presented by" with the sponsor's name and mark on tagged items in the app, the sponsor strip on Home that
  opens that sponsor's quests, and the proofs that a sponsor has no login, no link and no player data (FR-1304).
- **T-507b (blocked on Q-149):** the sponsor's logo and the category's banner are organizer uploads, and there is no storage bucket or
  upload path yet. Until then the app shows a lettered mark where the logo will go; a column pair on `sponsor` (logo, banner) comes with
  the upload and moves no tag.
- **T-507c:** tagging souvenirs. A souvenir's item shows on the owner's profile and recap, and the public item DTOs are source-free by
  design (T-402b); showing a sponsor there needs its own look at those DTOs and the erasure and export notes. Why split: quests,
  categories and rewards ship value alone; the souvenir tag touches `souvenir_rule`, frozen grant identity and private recipient
  provenance.
- FR-1306 (analytics and exports filtered by sponsor, Should) is not in this task's list; it waits for the analytics pages.

## Data (deny by default: RLS forced, no grant, doors only)

- `sponsor`: names in both languages (one is enough), `tier` (platinum, gold, silver, bronze, partner), `booth` (40), at most 100 per event.
  No contact, link or player column, and a test holds the column list.
- `sponsor_tag`: one row per tagged item, a `kind` with exactly one of `quest_id`, `category_id`, `reward_id` (same-event composite keys,
  ON DELETE CASCADE) and a unique index per kind, so an item has one sponsor; tagging it to another moves it. The quest, category and reward
  tables and every door that writes them are untouched.

## Doors

Team (owner/editor inside the event limit, one advisory lock per event for every write): `sponsor_authorize`, `sponsor_list`,
`sponsor_save`, `sponsor_delete`, `sponsor_tag_set`. Player: `member_sponsors` (active member; a tag is listed only on an item the
member may see: `member_sees_quest`, `sees_category`, an active reward in the member's audience). Routes: `GET/POST …/sponsors`,
`PUT/DELETE …/sponsors/{id}`, `PUT …/sponsor-tags`, and the player's `GET /v1/events/{id}/sponsors`.

## Care points

- A hidden quest, a category the player has not found and a reward outside their audience are never named in the player's tags (a test
  reads the answer for their ids).
- Two writes tagging one item both succeed and leave one row (the second moves it); a deleted sponsor, quest, category or reward takes
  its tags with it; a reward removed from the shop is no longer listed on the sponsor.
- "Tapping a sponsor lists its quests" (FR-1307): the quests tagged to it and every quest of a category tagged to it.
- No ad SDK, no link out, no tracking; nothing is written when a player completes a tagged quest (no join to a player anywhere).
