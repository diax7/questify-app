# T-706 - Message templates with CST rules, and the support contact (plan note, tier B)

Delivers FR-1780 and FR-1731 and the T-110 and T-407 notes on T-706. Migration **0127** (0125 and 0126 may be taken by parallel
work). ADR-460 to ADR-462, Q-420 to Q-422 (Proposed). The rest of the old T-706 is split off as **T-706b** (ADR-460).

## The split

T-706 as written holds four independent features: templates, push to a group (FR-1777), promotional campaigns, and the support
contact. Push to a group is a new `notice_kind` pair, a per-player fan-out with a cursor for the city and interest audience, a
console composer and the push worker's rendering; campaigns add the promotional email sender (a new outside call), the weekly cap
and scheduling. Each is a task of its own size. Done in one worker they would carry no review of the parts that matter.

- **T-706 (this)**: the four templates an admin edits and the CST rules enforced on save; the support contact and everything that
  shows it. "A promotional template without an opt-out cannot be saved." holds in the shared package, the API, the door and the
  table.
- **T-706b**: push to a group of players (the whole event, or opted-in players by city or interest, through `marketing_audience`
  and the existing push pipeline), promotional campaigns, the promotional email sender and the campaign-cap decision, and the
  senders reading their template slot (login code, promotional SMS, email, push).

## Design

1. **`message_template`**: four rows, seeded with today's copy: `login_code_sms`, `promo_sms`, `promo_email`, `promo_push`. Title
   (email subject, push title) and body in both languages. Table CHECKs keep the floor for every writer: `{optOutUrl}` in both
   bodies of `promo_sms` and `promo_email`, `{code}` in both of `login_code_sms`, a title exactly on email and push.
2. **CST rules** live once, in `packages/shared/src/messaging/templates.ts` (`templateIssues`): both languages written, in their
   own script; an SMS names Questify; a login-code SMS says it is a sign-in code (purpose); a promotional SMS or email holds
   the opt-out link; only the placeholders a template is given (`{code}`, `{optOutUrl}`; a push takes none); SMS within 3 parts
   counted with placeholders at their real length; length caps. The API runs it before the door and on a revert; the door
   re-checks the structure (placeholders, lengths, control characters) so a caller that skips the API is refused too.
3. **`admin_message_templates`, `admin_message_template_save`**: the catalog pattern (`console_admin()`, `catalog_audit`,
   `catalog_revert_doc`, a no-op save writes nothing). Console page `/console/messages`, Templates tab, with the reason under
   each refused box and the SMS part count.
4. **`support_contact`**: one row (WhatsApp, phone, email, each optional, international numbers, a plain email pattern).
   `support_contact_public()` is the one public read (`GET /v1/support-contact`, cached a minute); `admin_support_contact`,
   `admin_support_contact_save` (audited before and after). Console tab Support contact.
5. **Where it shows**: `/{lang}/contact` lists what is set (WhatsApp link `wa.me`, `tel:`, `mailto:`), else "being prepared";
   the legal pages put the support email into the drafts' `{{CONTACT_EMAIL}}` and `{{PRIVACY_CONTACT}}` placeholders (the rest wait
   for counsel, T-807).
6. **Deny by default**: both tables closed (RLS forced, no grant); five doors granted to the API role, four helpers closed;
   outsider, cross-tenant and route-table entries added.
