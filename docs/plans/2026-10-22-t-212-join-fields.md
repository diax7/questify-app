# T-212 — Join fields, guest list, company-email lock, welcome reward

T-212 delivers FR-129 to FR-137 and FR-1708 (partial, below). **Max bar:** it changes `join_event` (tenant isolation,
sign-in identity) and writes the points ledger (core). Migration **0028** (`0028_join_fields.sql`); 0026 (T-204/205)
and 0027 (T-210) are built in parallel. T-210 adds a status trigger on `enrollment` (`QL409`, hard limit) and does
not touch `join_event`; this plan replaces `join_event`, so its trigger covers both join paths unchanged.

## Scope

- **In:** up to 5 join fields per event (FR-129); the card shows them and the join needs every required one (FR-130);
  answers readable only by that event's team and the player (FR-131); a guest list of phones and emails (FR-132);
  "You're invited" with a join without a code (FR-133); guest-list-only events (FR-134); email-domain lock (FR-135);
  the welcome reward in the ledger, counted in score and balance, with the coin animation (FR-136, FR-137).
- **Out:** FR-1708 targeting itself (a category or reward limited by a join answer or a guest tag): it filters
  `member_event_quests`, `shop_rewards` and must refuse in `complete_quest` and `reserve_reward`, which T-205 and T-210
  are changing in parallel; the answers it needs are built here. Moved to a new line under T-212 in TASKS (follow-up
  after 0026/0027 merge). Guest-list tags go with it (no tag column now). Editing answers after joining (no FR).
  Export of answers is F-207 (T-6xx). The approval of a pending join is T-218; it must call the welcome grant.

## Decisions (tactical; Proposed ADR-145 to ADR-148)

1. **Fields** (`event_join_field`): `kind` in full_name, company, job_title, department, ticket_number, custom; a
   custom field has a label (ar and/or en, ≤ 60), the others none (their labels are the app's words); `required`;
   `position` 0–4; at most one of each non-custom kind; at most 5 (trigger, backstopping the API's count under the
   event lock). The team (owner and editor, event scope) reads and writes through RLS, like rewards; the API saves the
   whole list in one call (`PUT …/join-fields`): rows kept by id are updated, new ones inserted, missing ones deleted
   (their answers go with them, CASCADE), audited as one entry. Changing fields does not ask members to answer again.
2. **Answers** (`join_answer`, PK enrollment + field): text 1–200 after trim; written only by the join definers (no
   write grant); read by the event's team (RLS, event scope), the player themself and the admin; never by another
   organizer or player (cross-tenant probes for both seats). Rejoining after leaving replaces them.
3. **Guest list** (`event_guest`): `phone` (+9665XXXXXXXX) or `email` (lower case), unique per event, at most 10 000
   an event; the team uploads (paste or file text, up to 2 000 lines a call) and removes; invalid lines are reported
   back by line and not added (FR-132). Shown masked (phone last 4, email first letter + domain). The audit keeps
   counts, never a contact. A guest is matched against the player's verified contact (`player_contact`, both
   identifiers, ADR-075).
4. **Gates, any access type** (ADR-145): `event.guest_list_only` and `event.email_domains` (≤ 10, exact domains, the
   exclusion list's shape) apply whatever the access type; the SRS phrases them for private events, but tying them to
   it would block a change of access type (FR-1820). A guest passes both gates (the organizer named them) and joins
   `active` even in a private event (FR-1819). Refusals: `join_invite_only` (403), `join_domain_required` (403,
   `details.domains`).
5. **Email leaves the domain** (T-108 note, ADR-146): the lock is a gate at the join only; a member whose email
   changes later stays a member. Re-checking would need a pause state and a trigger on contact changes for a rare case.
6. **Welcome reward** (ADR-147): `event.welcome_points` (1–100 000, NULL = off), set by owner or editor, locked from
   live on (the spend-rule guard pattern, QE409) so every player of a live event gets the same amount. Ledger kind
   `welcome`, actor `system`, `score_delta = balance_delta > 0`, no references; one per enrollment (partial unique
   index), granted when an enrollment first becomes `active` (a rejoin gets nothing more). Pending joins get it at
   approval (T-218 calls `grant_welcome`). The join marks the event for ranking repair. The join answer carries
   `welcome` (points or 0) so the app plays the coin animation once.
7. **Card version** (ADR-148): the card bumps to `0.2` and lists "The answers you give below" when the event has
   fields; old apps get `card_version_stale`. Fields, gates and the welcome amount come with the card from a new
   anonymous definer `public_event_join_rules(code)` (no change to `public_event`).
8. **Invitations:** `my_invitations()` (player seat): scheduled or live events whose guest list holds one of the
   player's verified contacts and where they are not a member (a player who left is shown again), with fields, newest
   start first, at most 20. `join_invited(event, card, answers)`: via `invite` (new `joined_via` value), not found
   unless invited (one answer). No join code is returned.

## Database (0028)

- Tables above, RLS forced, `REVOKE ALL FROM PUBLIC`, organizer filled by `set_organizer_from_event()`.
- Event columns + grants + `event_welcome_guard` trigger.
- Ledger: kind `welcome`, shape CHECK, `ledger_entry_system_returns_only` widened to `redeem_return` or `welcome`.
- `join_event(code, via, card, answers jsonb)` replaces the 3-argument one (dropped): the shared core
  `join_core(target event, via, card, answers)` (not granted) runs: existing row locked → already / not_allowed;
  gates; answers checked (`JA422`, DETAIL `field:<id>` or `shape`); insert or rejoin; answers saved; welcome granted.
  Returns `outcome, enrollment_id, event_id, status, welcome`.
- `join_invited`, `my_invitations`, `public_event_join_rules`, `grant_welcome(enrollment)` (internal, not granted).
- Outsider suite is catalog-driven; cross-tenant probes for the three tables (organizer and player seats).

## API

- Card: `GET /v1/public/events/{code}` adds `join: {fields, guestListOnly, emailDomains, welcomePoints}`.
- `POST /v1/join` takes `answers` (object, ≤ 5 entries) and `eventId` instead of `joinCode` when `via: invite`;
  refusals map JA422 → 422 `invalid_join_answers` (`details.field`), `invite_only`, `domain_required`.
- `GET /v1/me/invitations`.
- Dashboard: `GET/PUT …/events/{eventId}/join-fields`, `GET/POST/DELETE …/events/{eventId}/guests`,
  `PUT …/events/{eventId}/join-rules` (guestListOnly, emailDomains, welcomePoints; `setting_locked` from live).
- Players list (`GET …/players`) adds `answers: [{fieldId, value}]` read under RLS for the page's enrollments.

## Web dashboard

A "Joining" page per event: the fields editor (kind, label for custom, required, order, ≤ 5), the rules (guest list
only, domains, welcome points), the guest list (paste lines, see the invalid ones, masked list, remove). The players
list shows answers per player.

## App

The card renders the fields (inputs, required marks, the app points to the first empty required one), the gate text
("By invitation", "Needs an email at …"), the welcome amount; answers ride with a join that waits for sign-in (held in
memory, never in route params). Home shows "You're invited" cards (`GET /v1/me/invitations`) that open the card for
that event and join without a code. A join with `welcome > 0` plays the coin-to-balance celebration. The history names
`welcome` entries.

## CTO plan review (opus): 86, approve with revisions; folded in

1. **Welcome lock:** `welcome_points` is locked once the event has any enrollment or is live, whichever comes first
   (a scheduled event takes joins), so every member gets the same amount.
2. **Fields save is versioned:** `event.join_fields_version`; `PUT …/join-fields` sends the version it read, a
   mismatch is 409 `join_fields_changed` (a stale tab cannot delete fields and their answers).
3. **Welcome by trigger:** an AFTER INSERT OR UPDATE OF status trigger on `enrollment` grants it whenever a row
   becomes `active` (join, invite, the T-218 approval, a lift), `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING` on the per-enrollment index;
   the join reads back whether this join granted it. A concurrency test: two first joins make one entry.
4. **Invite path:** bypasses `join_methods` (the guest list is its own way in); every other check of `join_core`
   runs (ended, banned or removed, T-210's limit). `joined_via` gains `invite`.
5. An optional field left blank is left out (a blank string is refused); an answer keyed by another event's field is
   an unknown field.
6. DATA_MODEL updated to the new tables; Q-115 (guest-list purge after the event, answers on account deletion).
7. The module stays `apps/api/src/join/` (new files beside `join.ts`), mutation-tested there.
8. The 0.2 card applies to every event (pre-launch).
9. Outsider/anonymous: `public_event_join_rules` returns fields, gates and amount only; `my_invitations` lists only
   the caller's.
10. FR-1842 (who sees the full name on the card) stays out of scope; the guest exemption is FR-1819's invited half.
