# T-306 — Live polls (and the split for community quests)

T-306 as listed delivers thirteen FRs across two features: live polls (F-100: FR-239 to FR-242) and community quests
(F-101: FR-243 to FR-250, FR-1620). **This plan builds the live polls** and fixes the design of community quests, which
move to T-306b and T-306c (ADR-219). **Core bar**: a poll pays points through `vote_quest` and the completion engine,
and T-306b's payer pays many players at once: one Opus plan review, one Opus code review, mutation on GitHub.
Migration **0052** (0051 is T-305c's, undeployed); T-306b takes **0053**. Depends on T-302 (`vote` type, `quest_vote`,
`vote_quest`, `member_vote`, `quest_vote_counts`), T-211 (rehearsal), T-220 (audiences), all done. CranL runs through
0047, so 0041's `quest_vote` is changed by a new migration, never edited.

Dhia is away: the open choices are my recommendation, written as **Proposed** ADR-219 to ADR-223 and Q-183 to Q-187.

**CTO plan review 2026-10-24: 84/100, findings folded in** (1: live polls are left out of every category rule, since
"no reader changes" was false; 2: the guard trigger reads the new row, refuses adding or removing `live` on a
visible or sent quest, and locks `choices` and `minutes` on send; 3: `serverNow` is outside the ETag; 4 and 5, for
T-306b: the settle lag is enforced, not assumed, and an owner can settle and pay at once, so results never wait
forever; 6: `live_state` uses the quest open checks and caps `closesAt` at the event end; 7: an empty answer straight
away and a short cache; 8: percentages of voters, the wipe report counts votes per quest; 9: the deploy note; 10:
T-306b's tables closed with outsider tests and a payer crossing a meet payment on two connections).

## The split (ADR-219)

- **T-306 (this plan):** live polls (FR-239 to FR-242): a vote quest marked live, one or several choices, points
  optional, Send and Close from the dashboard, the in-app pop-up card, results after voting, at close or never, and
  the live-state endpoint the app polls. The poll scene on the big screen is T-504's; the poll push is T-501's.
- **T-306b:** community quests (FR-243 to FR-246, FR-248 to FR-250, FR-1620, and FR-247's points, "a real-world
  moment" and "nothing"): the goal and its milestones, the settled tally, contributors, the batched points payer, the
  progress bar on the event home and the dashboard. Design fixed below (ADR-222, ADR-223); its worker writes its own
  plan from it. The bar on the big screen is T-504's; the milestone push is T-501's.
- **T-306c:** FR-247's unlock rewards: a quest, category or shop reward that stays locked until a community step is
  reached. It adds a gate to `quest_open_check`, `category_lock`, `reserve_reward` and every reader of them, which is
  its own review (as T-304 split its gates from its bonus).

Why: polls are a small change on a finished type; the community payer is a new kind of payer (many players in one
step) with its own lock, batch and idempotency rules; the unlocks touch every quest and shop reader.

## Live polls (ADR-220)

**A live poll is a vote quest with a `live` block.** It sits in a category like any quest, so points, the ledger, XP,
audiences, the repeat cap, rehearsal, erasure, flags and removal all apply unchanged. The block (shared registry, zod,
and a CHECK in 0052):

| Field | Values | FR |
|---|---|---|
| `live.choices` | `one` \| `several` | FR-240 |
| `live.results` | `after_vote` \| `at_close` \| `never` | FR-242 |
| `live.minutes` | integer 1 to 1,440: how long it stays open once sent | FR-239 |

`quest.points` is the poll's points; 0 is a poll without points (FR-241): the vote is recorded as a 0-point
completion, as any 0-point quest today. XP follows the quest rule unchanged (the first completion of a quest pays
`quest_xp()`), so a 0-point poll still gives XP (Q-184). A vote quest without `live` keeps T-302's behaviour, now with
`several` not allowed (a plain vote stays one choice).

**Several choices (FR-240).** `quest_vote` becomes one row per chosen option: 0052 drops its primary key
`(enrollment_id, quest_id)` for `(enrollment_id, quest_id, option_id)`, and `completion_id` loses `UNIQUE` (an index
stays; several rows share one completion). `vote_quest(target uuid, options text[], request_key text)` replaces the
one-option signature (dropped, `REVOKE ALL … FROM PUBLIC` and the grant restated): a non-empty array of distinct, known
option ids (`VO422` otherwise), exactly one unless the poll is `several` (`VC422 too_many_choices`). The replay compares
the stored set with the sent set (`QK422` on a difference). `member_vote` (`mine` per option), `quest_vote_counts`
(rows per option; plus `voters` = distinct enrollments), erasure and the rehearsal wipe already work per row; each is
re-read and its tests gain a two-option vote. One completion, one payment, whatever the number of choices.

**Send and Close.** The poll's open window is the quest's own `visible`, `opens_at` and `closes_at`, so every player
reader and `quest_open_check` already hide it before it is sent and refuse it after it closes.

**Live polls stay out of the category rules** (CTO 1). A poll is open for minutes, so counting it in a category would
make the completion bonus unreachable for everyone who missed it, block an in-order category's next quests while it
is open, and list a poll in `/live` that the in-order gate then refuses. 0052 restates `category_done` and
`category_cleared` to skip live polls (a category of only live polls is never "done", so it pays no bonus and
unlocks nothing), and `category_lock`'s order check skips them both ways: a live poll is never the earlier quest that
blocks, and never itself blocked by an unfinished earlier quest. Its category's `opens_at`, `unlock_after`, hidden
reveal and audience still apply (a poll in a locked category is neither listed nor playable). The builder refuses a
live poll in a refreshing category (played once, as T-304c's rule for votes). Tests for each case.

- Saved as a draft: a live poll is always saved with `visible = false` and no window. A guard trigger,
  `quest_live_poll_guard` (BEFORE INSERT OR UPDATE, from the API role's seat), checks the **new row's values**, not
  the columns named (CTO 2; `visible` defaults to true, so an insert that omits it is caught): a live poll with
  `poll_sent_at` null must have `visible = false` and no window; once sent, `visible`, `opens_at` and `closes_at` may
  not change. It also refuses adding `live` to a vote quest that is visible or has votes, removing `live` from a sent
  poll, and changing `live.choices` or `live.minutes` once sent (`PL409 {field}`). The builder hides those fields.
- `send_poll(target_event, target_quest, request)` (owner or editor, definer): the event live, or in rehearsal for a
  rehearsal-phase send; sets `visible = true`, `opens_at = now()`, `closes_at = least(now() + minutes, event end)`, `poll_sent_at`
  (new column, no grant) and audits `quest.poll_send`. Sending twice answers the first send (idempotent; `PS409
  already_sent` only if it was closed). A poll's options and its `live` block lock on send (T-302's
  `quest_lock_played`, extended to "sent", ADR-194, and the guard above).
- `close_poll(…)`: `closes_at = now()` if later; audited; a second close is a no-op.
- Rehearsal: a poll sent before go-live is a test send. The go-live wipe (`wipe_rehearsal`, restated in 0052) deletes
  its rehearsal votes as today and puts the poll back to draft (`visible = false`, window and `poll_sent_at` null), so
  it can be sent for real.

**Results (FR-242).** `poll_results(target uuid)` (player, definer): the options with their counts and `voters`, only
when the policy allows: `after_vote` once the caller voted, `at_close` once `closes_at <= now()`, `never` never
(`PR403 results_hidden {when}`). Same membership and audience checks as `member_vote`. The team keeps
`quest_vote_counts` at any time (ADR-195). Counts never name anyone. With several choices, each option's share is
out of `voters` (distinct players), not out of rows, so shares can add up to more than 100% (CTO 8; the app and the
dashboard say "of {voters} voters"). The rehearsal wipe's report counts `votes` as distinct (player, quest), not
rows (`wipe_rehearsal`, 0050:942, restated here).

## Live updates (ADR-221)

`GET /v1/events/{id}/stream` (server-sent events) is in API.md but was never built; nothing in the app or API uses
it. Polls do not need it: **the app polls one small endpoint**, and the stream stays a later step (ARCHITECTURE's
growth path) that T-504 or a load test can call for.

- `GET /v1/events/{id}/live` (player, active member) → `{polls: [{questId, closesAt}], closed: [{questId}],
  community: null, serverNow}`, from one definer, `live_state(target)`: the live polls open now that the caller may
  play and has not voted in, by the same predicates as `quest_open_check` and `member_event_quests` (CTO 6: the
  quest's audience, its category's `opens_at`, `unlock_after` and hidden reveal, the operator's play right; the
  in-order rule never applies to a poll); `closesAt` is never past the event end; `closed`: live polls with `at_close` results that closed in
  the last 15 minutes and the caller could see. `community` is filled by T-306b. Weak ETag over the body **without
  `serverNow`** (CTO 3: it changes every call), `304` when unchanged; `serverNow` is sent on a 304 too, in a
  `Server-Now` header (the app reads either). Limit 30 a minute per player.
- Cost: the event's open live polls are few (one or two at once) and the query reads them by `(event_id)` with the
  vote check by the primary key. The API keeps each event's open-poll list (ids, windows, audiences) in memory for
  3 s, and when the event has no open live poll and no community goal it answers at once without the per-player
  query (CTO 7). At 5,000 players every 10 s that is 500 small calls a second, inside the pool's
  budget. An index `quest_live_idx ON quest (event_id) WHERE type = 'vote' AND type_config ? 'live'`.
- The app calls it every `livePollSeconds` (tuning, default 10) while an event screen is open and the app is active,
  once on return to active, and never in the background. A failed call is retried at the next interval, silently.

## T-306b — community quests: the design fixed here (ADR-222, ADR-223)

**The goal.** `community_goal` (event, title ar/en, `measure` `points` | `completions` | `players`, optional
`quest_id` or `category_id` for the last two, `target`, `starts_at`, `ends_at`, `paid_to` `contributors` |
`everyone`, cached `progress`, `status` `scheduled` | `running` | `reached` | `missed`). `community_step` (goal,
`threshold` 1..target, the goal itself the step at `target`; up to 5 milestones; `reward` `points` (1..100,000) |
`moment` (organizer text ar/en) | `nothing`; T-306c adds `unlock`). The event switch `config.features.community`
(platform and event, as `miniGames`). Windows of one event never overlap (checked under the event's advisory lock, as
rank prizes), so the home shows one bar. Everything but the text locks once `starts_at` passes.

**Counting (FR-243 to FR-245) without a hot row.** Nothing in the scan transaction touches the goal: at 20,000
completions a minute one shared counter row would serialise every scan in the event. Instead a **community tick**
(its own timer, every 5 s, jittered; several instances harmless: each goal is taken `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`) keeps a
**settled tally**: `settled_until` and `settled_value` on the goal. Each run adds the slice `[settled_until, now() −
30 s)` to the settled value and moves `settled_until`, then shows `progress = settled + the open tail [settled_until,
now())`. The 30 s lag covers transactions still open (statement timeout 5 s, lock timeout 2 s), so a slice is read
once its rows can no longer appear. Each run reads at most about a minute of rows, by `ledger_entry_event_idx`
`(event_id, created_at)` and `quest_completion_quest_idx`.

**The lag is enforced, not assumed** (CTO 4): today only `statement_timeout` (5 s) and `lock_timeout` (2 s) are set
(`server.ts:63,66`), and a transaction of many statements can stay open longer. The tick settles only up to
`least(now() - 30 s, the oldest xact_start of any open transaction on the database)` (read from
`pg_stat_activity` through a definer, the tick's own transaction left out), so a slice is settled only once no
transaction that began inside it is still open. Belt and braces: `idle_in_transaction_session_timeout` of 10 s on
the API's connections (an idle transaction is a bug). A goal closes at `least(ends_at, event end)` plus the larger
of 15 s and the lag, and only once the settled point has passed its end.

- `points`: `score_delta` of `quest` and `bonus` entries, and of `removal` entries reversing one, each counted at its
  own `created_at` inside the window (a removal lowers progress; awards, welcome, redemptions and community rewards
  never count). Nothing is taken from anyone (FR-244).
- `completions`: completions of the quest (or the category's quests), minus removals of their ledger entries.
- `players`: the number of contributors.
- Rehearsal entries never count; community quests do not run in rehearsal (the dashboard shows a preview; Q-186).
- **Contributors**: `community_contributor (goal_id, enrollment_id, first_at)`, filled from each slice (`INSERT …
  SELECT DISTINCT … ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`): whoever has a counted row in the window. It is the `players` measure and
  the default reward list (FR-248).

**Reaching and closing.** When progress (settled plus tail) reaches a step, the tick writes `community_reach (step_id,
reached_at)` once (primary key) and, for a `points` step, freezes the list of whom to pay as `community_payout
(step_id, enrollment_id, status due | paid | skipped, entry_id)`: the contributors, or every active enrollment for
`everyone` (players and operators who may play; not banned, left or pending), in one `INSERT … SELECT`, no enrollment
locked. A player who joins later is not on the list. Reached stays reached, even if a later removal lowers progress.
At `least(ends_at, event end) + 15 s` the tick settles the whole window and closes the goal: `reached` if the goal step
was reached, else `missed`, and a missed goal pays nothing and shows "not reached" (FR-249). Milestones reached before
a miss keep their rewards (FR-246).

**The payer (ADR-223).** Pays `due` rows in batches, each batch its own transaction:

1. Take up to 500 due rows of one step `ORDER BY enrollment_id FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` (only the tick touches payout
   rows; SKIP LOCKED never waits, so two instances split the work).
2. Lock the batch's enrollments **in one statement, in id order** (`SELECT … WHERE id = ANY(…) ORDER BY id FOR NO KEY
   UPDATE`), before any other lock (rules.md lock order, ADR-211). No item, no advisory lock, and **no XP**: a
   community reward is ledger kind `community`, and XP follows only `quest` entries (0039), so the XP-row order rule
   has nothing to order. A later reward that pays XP or items must take XP rows in player id order and items after the
   enrollments.
3. For each locked row still payable (active, may play, results not confirmed): one ledger entry, kind `community`,
   actor `system`, `score_delta = points`, `balance_delta = points` while the shop is open else 0 (as `bonus`), a new
   `community_step_id` column with a partial unique index `(community_step_id, enrollment_id) WHERE kind =
   'community'`: the database itself refuses a second payment. The payout row turns `paid` with its entry, or
   `skipped`. Then `mark_ranking_repair(event)` once per batch (the rebuild job moves the ranking within seconds;
   no Redis write per player).
4. Idempotency: the payout row's status and the unique index; a batch that fails rolls back whole and its rows stay
   `due` for the next run. Retry: a deadlock or lock timeout (40P01, 55P03; lock timeout 2 s) is logged and retried on
   the next tick, with no client involved. A player's own request that waits on a batch past the lock timeout gets the
   existing `503 busy` with `Retry-After`, and the app retries with the same idempotency key (no double pay).
5. Budget: each run pays batches until 2 s are spent; a timing test pays 10,000 players within 30 s on the CI runner
   and holds one batch under 250 ms (the longest a scan can wait on it). Batch size and budget are tuning (Q-187).

`ledger_entry_kind_check`, the `system` actor check and `remove_award` (a community entry is removable like a bonus)
gain `community`. `confirm_results` refuses `409 community_paying` while a goal of the event is not closed or has due
rows, so final results include every community reward. It can never wait forever (CTO 5): the answer offers
**Settle and pay now** (owner, `settle_community(event)`), which closes every goal whose end has passed, settles it
inline and runs the payer to the end in batches in the same request's loop (each batch its own transaction, the
same lock order), then the owner confirms again; due rows older than 2 minutes raise an operations alert (the
tick is stuck); and turning the `community` switch off closes the running goals at once (`missed` unless already
reached; reached steps still pay). Erasure keeps the rows (they name an enrollment, which stays as
a tombstone).

**Where it shows (FR-250).** `live_state.community` = `{goalId, title, measure, target, progress, steps: [{threshold,
reward, reachedAt}], status, endsAt}`, read from the cached columns (no aggregate per request); the event home's bar
(a wooden bar filling with gold, step markers, "Reached!" or "Not reached"), the dashboard's goal page. A reached step
with a `moment` shows its text as a card once.

**Deny by default and tests (CTO 10).** `community_goal`, `community_step`, `community_reach`,
`community_contributor` and `community_payout` each get RLS enabled and forced and `REVOKE ALL … FROM PUBLIC` in the
file that creates them, no grant (definers are the doors), classified in the cross-tenant suite, covered by the
outsider suite. A crossed-lock test on two connections (as `test/db/meet.int.test.ts`, rules.md lock order): a payer
batch and a meet payment over the same two players, in both orders, never deadlock, or a deadlock turns into a
retried batch and a `503 busy` the client retries, with nobody paid twice. The milestone push is a T-501 note (send once per reached step to the
active members); the big-screen scene a T-504 note.

## Proposed decisions and questions

- **ADR-219** T-306 splits in three: T-306 live polls (FR-239 to FR-242); T-306b community quests (FR-243 to FR-246,
  FR-248 to FR-250, FR-1620, FR-247 but unlocks); T-306c unlock rewards (FR-247's unlocks). The poll and community
  pushes move to T-501, the big-screen scenes to T-504. Q-183.
- **ADR-220** A live poll is a vote quest with a `live` block (one or several choices, results after voting, at close
  or never, open 1 to 1,440 minutes once sent, never past the event end); its window is the quest's own
  `visible`/`opens_at`/`closes_at`, set only by Send and Close (a guard on the new row's values; `live` and its
  choices and minutes locked once sent); live polls are left out of every category rule; several choices are one completion and one payment; a rehearsal send resets at go-live.
  Q-184.
- **ADR-221** Live updates are polled: one `GET /v1/events/{id}/live` every 10 s (tuning) while an event screen is
  open and the app active, with an ETag that leaves out `serverNow`; the server-sent stream in API.md is not built and stays a growth step. Q-185.
- **ADR-222** Community quests count from a settled tally kept by a 5-second tick (a 30 s settle lag, nothing written
  in the scan transaction; settled only past the oldest open transaction), contributors kept as rows, the payee list frozen when a step is reached, one running goal
