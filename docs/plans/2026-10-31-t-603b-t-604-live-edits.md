# T-603b and T-604: pause and skip, live points edits (future or past), delete keep or remove, change sets (plan)

**Tier A** for T-603b (it changes what the completion engine accepts and who may reach later quests) and for the points
part of T-604 (FR-411, FR-412, FR-413: compensating ledger entries). **Tier B slice, same worker, no mutation:** the
confirmation-screen sweep (FR-1031, FR-1032) and change sets (FR-1034, FR-1035): they write no points themselves; a
change-set item that edits points calls the tier A door unchanged. Plan, plan review, build, one code review, then one
mutation run for the tier A TypeScript. Proposed at build time (next free numbers when built): **ADR-386** (T-603b),
**ADR-387** (T-604 points), **ADR-388** (confirmations and change sets), **Q-353 to Q-355** (texts at the end).

**Migrations (all new; staging runs through 0096 once T-603 deploys, so 0096 and earlier are frozen and restated):**
**0097** T-603b, **0098** T-604 points, **0099** change sets. Three commits at least, in that order.

## The one path for points (reused; nothing writes points another way)

Every points move stays one `INSERT INTO ledger_entry` inside a definer door, in the transaction of the fact it pays
for; the triggers do the rest (`ledger_a_stamp_rehearsal`, `apply_ledger_entry` for score, balance and
`score_version`, `ledger_operator_play_guard`, `ledger_xp` on quest and removal only, `ledger_streak` on quest only,
the append-only guard), and a door outside a quest completion calls `ranking_repair_mark(ev.id)` so the repair tick
rebuilds the boards from the ledger. **Removing points is `remove_award`'s write**, factored into one internal
`write_removal(ev, credit, why, me)` that `remove_award` and the quest delete both call (one way to write a removal).
The only new writer is the kind `adjust` below, written by one door; T-603b writes no points at all.

## T-603b: pause a quest, skip a broken step (migration 0097)

**Data.** `quest.paused_at timestamptz`, `quest.paused_by uuid`, `quest.skipped_at timestamptz`, `quest.skipped_by uuid`
(staff named without FK, as `ledger_entry.staff_user_id`). A trigger keeps "skipped only in an in-order category": a
quest moved to another category, or a category switched to any order, clears `skipped_at`.

**Doors** (definer, `award_event_of` = owner or editor of the organizer inside the editor's event scope, so
SQL-enforced; REVOKE from PUBLIC, GRANT to `questify_app` only):
- `set_quest_paused(target_event, target_quest, paused boolean, request uuid) → jsonb {paused, changed}`: quest in the
  event (AQ404), event not ended or archived (QE409 `event_closed`); open from rehearsal on, as the room (ADR-385).
  Setting the state it already has answers `changed:false` and writes nothing (natural idempotency: a retry is
  harmless; no key needed). Audit `quest.pause` / `quest.resume` with `before`/`after`.
- `set_step_skipped(target_event, target_quest, skipped boolean, request uuid) → jsonb {skipped, changed}`: as above,
  plus the quest must be in an in-order category and be neither a flash quest nor a live poll (they are already outside
  the order) → SP422 `not_in_order`. Audit `quest.skip` / `quest.unskip`.

**What pause does.** `quest_open_check` (restated from 0055) refuses `stored.paused_at IS NOT NULL` with QN403
DETAIL `paused`, right after the `hidden` check. Every start door already calls it (record_completion for scan, code,
NFC, kiosk, screen QR, location, vote, survey, share, challenge and manual awards; `submit_quest`, `play_enter` for
quiz and vote, `stamp_step`, `meet_may_play`), so one line covers all. Work already started finishes and pays:
`pay_completion` paths (approving a submission sent before the pause, a quiz attempt finishing) do not call the check
(Proposed, Q-353). A hand award of a paused quest is refused too (one rule; resume to award).
- **A scan in flight.** The scan does not lock against the pause: the pause's commit is the cut-off. A scan whose
  `quest_open_check` ran before the pause committed pays; any check after it refuses. No completion is ever half-made
  (one transaction).
- **A retried scan.** `record_completion` looks up `(enrollment, idempotency_key)` before `quest_open_check`, so a
  retry of a key that already paid answers its first result (`replayed`) even while paused. A key refused `paused`
  stored nothing in the database; but the HTTP idempotency store remembers 4xx answers for 24 hours, so the same key
  replays 403 `paused` after resume. The app makes a new key per scan, so a new scan after resume pays. Both tested.
- **Order and bonus.** A paused step in an in-order category still holds the steps after it (the confirmation says so
  and points to Skip). It still counts toward the category being done.

**What skip does.** `category_rule_lock`, `category_done`, `category_cleared` (restated from 0055) leave out
`q.skipped_at IS NOT NULL` exactly where they leave out flash quests and live polls: the step no longer holds later
steps, the category's bonus or a category unlocked after it. The step stays visible and completable (a player who can
still do it earns its points); pause it as well to close it. **Skip pays nobody** (DASHBOARD 3.8's "award or not award"
branch is dropped: Proposed, Q-353; the team uses Award to a group if they want to compensate). Unskip puts it back in
the chain: players who moved on without it are held at their next step until they complete it; its confirmation says
how many (count from `control_done`).

**Rehearsal.** `wipe_rehearsal` (restated from 0062, two lines) clears `paused_*` and `skipped_*` at go-live, so a pause
tried in rehearsal never opens the live event paused; the audit rows stay. `duplicate_event` (0092): confirm it copies
by column list (new columns then start null); if it copies `paused_at`/`skipped_at`, restate it to leave them out.

**Reads.** `member_event_quests` (restated from 0068; DROP and CREATE, new column) adds `paused boolean`; the skip is
already reflected in `lock`. `control_done` (restated from 0096) adds `paused` and `skipped` and, for unskip, `behind`
(members past the step who have not done it). API: `QuestState` (`apps/api/src/home/home.ts`) gains `paused` after
`not_live`, before `locked`; `pickUpNext` and progress follow `isOpen`, so a paused quest is never Up next.
`POST …/events/{id}/quests/{questId}/pause|resume|skip|unskip` (owner, editor; 404 outside reach). QN403 `paused`
maps to `quest_not_open` reason `paused` everywhere the reason is mapped (player, operator, kiosk answers).

**UI.** Control room quest table: a Paused / Skipped badge per row and a "More" menu with Pause/Resume and Skip
step/Restore step (skip only on in-order quests), each through `ConfirmDialog` (ADR-063 parts: what will happen, what
will not change, Cancel focused). App: the quest card and detail show "Paused by the organizer" and no scan button; a
scan answered `paused` says so. Every string in Arabic and English (`packages/shared/src/i18n/messages.ts`), RTL.

## T-604 points: future or past, delete keep or remove (migration 0098)

**Eligible types for "also past"**: types paid `quest.points × boost multiplier` once per completion through
`record_completion` (scan_qr, enter_code, location, vote, survey, share, challenge, and hand awards of them).
Submission, quiz, passport, meet and game pay by their own amounts or paths; for them the screen offers only "future
completions" and says why (Proposed, Q-354). Before go-live there is no question: rehearsal completions are wiped.

**Data.** Ledger kind `adjust` (kind check restated from 0093 with `adjust` added; `ledger_entry_staff_kinds` restated
with `adjust`). New table `quest_points_change` (id, organizer, event, quest, staff_user_id, from_points, to_points,
completions, players, raised, lowered, clamped, idempotency_key, request_id, created_at; UNIQUE (event, staff, key);
FK quest ON DELETE RESTRICT; forced RLS, no grants). `ledger_entry.points_change_id uuid` FK to it. Shape:
`kind = 'adjust'` ⇔ `points_change_id IS NOT NULL`; an adjust has `completion_id`, `quest_id`, actor `staff`,
`staff_user_id`, `score_delta <> 0`, `balance_delta` of the same sign or 0, and no reverses, redemption, category,
session, community step or boost; `UNIQUE (points_change_id, completion_id)`. Not in the operator play guard, XP (a
flat amount per quest, ADR-180), streaks or `awarded_today` (operator caps); not removable on its own (AT422 stays).

**The rule: absolute targets, never edited rows.** For each *kept* completion of the quest (its `quest` entry has no
`removal`), of an active member, the target is `to_points × multiplier` (multiplier of the entry's `boost_id`, else
1). Its net is the sum of `score_delta` over the `quest` entry and every `adjust` with that `completion_id`. The door
writes one `adjust` of `target − net` where that is not zero. Consequences:
- **A second edit** recomputes from the ledger: 100→150 past writes +50, then 150→120 past writes −30. A mix of earlier
  future-only edits converges: every kept completion becomes worth the new points.
- **Undo** is another edit back with "also past"; there is no row to delete and nothing is ever updated.
- **A retry, or a run with a new key**, finds nets already at target and writes nothing new. The idempotency key
  (required: 422 `idempotency_key_required`) exists to replay the first answer with its counts (AK422 when the same key
  arrives with another quest or points).
- **Lowering** never takes the score or the balance below zero (FR-1601's rule, as `remove_award`): the entry takes
  `least(−delta, current score)` and `least(−delta, current balance)`; the screen counts the clamped players. No
  reserved-prize refusal (one player's reservation must not block an edit for all; Q-354).
- **Ranking ties.** Entries are written in the members' current `score_version` order, then completion id, so tied
  players keep their relative order (the ledger id is the tie-break, ADR-118). `ranking_repair_mark` once; the rebuild
  may send rank-diff pushes (T-503b), as any bulk award does.

**Door** `apply_past_points(target_event, target_quest, request_key, request uuid) → (change_id, completions,
players, raised, lowered, clamped, replayed)`: `award_event_of`; key check; `ensure_rehearsal_wiped`; replay lookup;
event live (AS409 otherwise) and no `event_result` (RF409); quest in the event and eligible (AQ404, PX422);
`SELECT … FROM quest … FOR UPDATE`; reads `to_points` from the row as written by this transaction; editor (not owner)
refused AC409 `each` when any raise per completion exceeds `award_cap_each` (Proposed: past raises are a bulk award in
effect, FR-1712); locks the affected enrollments in id order; writes the header, the adjusts, one audit row
`quest.points_past` (before `{points}`, after `{points, change, completions, players, raised, lowered, clamped}`).
**Route:** `PATCH …/quests/{questId}` gains `apply: "future" | "past"`. In a live event, when `points` changes and the
quest has kept real completions, `apply` is required (409 `apply_required`); `past` on an ineligible type → 422
`invalid_quest {field: "apply"}`. `future` is today's update. `past` runs `writeQuest` then the door in the same
`withEvent` transaction. **Preview:** `GET …/quests/{questId}/impact?points=N` (owner, editor) through a read-only
definer `quest_points_impact` → `{eligible, completions, players, raise, lower, clamped, prizesHanded}` for the
screen's real numbers (FR-413, FR-1032); `prizesHanded` counts rank prizes already handed over so the screen states
they are not taken back.

**Delete a played quest: keep or remove (FR-412).** A played quest cannot be a deleted row (completions, ledger and
history name it). `quest.deleted_at`, `deleted_by`: the delete sets `deleted_at` and `visible = false`. A trigger
refuses any later change to a deleted quest (QX409). Players: the hidden check shuts every door, locks and bonuses
skip invisible quests, history keeps the title. Staff reads leave deleted quests out: builder (`readBuilder`,
`readQuest`, reorder, `completionsOf`), `control_done`, the bulk award quest picker, QR and NFC pages (grep
`FROM quest` in `apps/api/src` and the latest SQL readers; the commit lists each as "filtered" or "left, because").
`duplicate_event` skips deleted quests. A category whose only quests are deleted still refuses deletion
(`has_completions`; FR-1004's category branch stays out, Q-354).
- `DELETE …/quests/{questId}` with real completions requires `points: "keep" | "remove"` (409 `points_choice_required`)
  and an idempotency key when `remove`. Unplayed quests keep today's hard delete; rehearsal-only stays
  `rehearsal_data`.
- Door `delete_played_quest(target_event, target_quest, remove boolean, reason text, request uuid)`: as above for
  reach, phase and `FOR UPDATE` on the quest; sets `deleted_at`; with `remove`, calls `write_removal` for every kept
  `quest` and `first_bonus` entry of the quest, any enrollment status, in `score_version` order. **`write_removal`
  of a `quest` entry takes the entry plus its completion's adjusts** (else a past raise would outlive the removal);
  clamped as today; it reverses the entry's XP (existing trigger) and cancels a reserved quest item (existing
  `credit_item`). Category bonuses and streak days already paid stay (Q-354). One audit row `quest.delete`
  (`after: {points: keep|remove, removed, total}`). A retry: the quest is deleted (404 from the builder read) and
  `ledger_entry_removal_key` makes a second removal of an entry impossible.
- `remove_award` keeps its checks and its own AO409; it now calls `write_removal`. Its answer for a quest entry
  includes the folded adjusts.

**Lock order (no new deadlock).** Rule: **event → quest → enrollment**. Today `record_completion` locks the enrollment
and only touches the quest through the ledger FK (KEY SHARE, after the enrollment). Without a change, a scan that read
100 before an edit commits could pay 100 after "also past" moved everyone to 150. So: `record_completion` (restated
from 0062) takes its **first** quest read `FOR KEY SHARE` (before the enrollment lock); FOR UPDATE in the past and
delete doors conflicts with it, so the door waits for scans already holding it (and adjusts them after they commit:
each plpgsql statement takes a new snapshot), and later scans wait and read the new points. Scans never block each
other (KEY SHARE vs KEY SHARE) nor a plain builder edit (NO KEY UPDATE). `remove_award` and `grant_manual_award`
(restated from 0093 and 0032) take the quest's KEY SHARE before their enrollment lock. Any path missed still cannot
corrupt: Postgres aborts one side of a deadlock, the API answers 503 busy (ADR-211) and the key makes the retry safe.

**Kind lists** gain `adjust` where lines show: `member_activity` ("Points updated: <quest>"), `member_ledger` (from→to
from the header); `rehearsal_card` left out (no rehearsal adjusts); the commit names each. **UI.** The quest editor,
on Save with changed points in a live event, opens the confirmation: real counts from `impact`, two radio rows
(neither pre-selected), "Rank prizes already handed over are not taken back" when `prizesHanded > 0`, confirm
disabled until a choice. Delete opens the keep or remove screen (DASHBOARD section 5's example). Every adjustment is
described as "a new entry in the points record".

## Tier B slice: confirmation sweep and change sets (migration 0099 for change sets)

**Sweep (FR-1031, FR-1032).** Map every row of DASHBOARD_AND_CONSOLE section 5's table, plus the app's log out, cancel
a reservation, leave an event and delete account, to its shipped screen; add a "Shipped in" column there. Build the
missing ones with `ConfirmDialog` (web) and the app's confirm sheet; each new one gets a browser or component test:
nothing happens on dismiss, Cancel has focus, real names and numbers shown.

**Change sets.** Tables `change_set` (id, organizer, event, created_by, status `open|applied|closed`, created_at) and
`change_set_item` (id, set, position, op `add|edit|delete`, target `quest|category|reward`, target_id, body jsonb (the
same body as the API operation, including `apply` and `points` choices), before jsonb, status
`pending|applied|failed|stale`, error jsonb, applied_at); forced RLS for owner and editor of the event, deny by
default, no PUBLIC grants, outsider test. Cap 100 items. Routes: `POST …/events/{id}/change-sets` (items),
`GET …/change-sets/{setId}` (each item validated now: a dry run of the same service function in a transaction that is
always rolled back, so the database's own checks speak; points items validated by `impact`, never by running the
points door; `invalid` with its refusal; `before` from the target's current audit view), `POST …/apply {itemIds}`
(each selected item through its service function in its own transaction, idempotency key `cs:<itemId>`, an item whose
target changed since `before` is `stale` and skipped), `POST …/close`. Each applied item writes its usual audit row, so
it appears in the change log. Dashboard: a "Change sets" review page (list, before/after, checkboxes, Apply selected
through a confirmation, Close). Creating sets is API-first (the future AI wizard, F-197); the page reviews them
(Proposed, Q-355). No post-commit effect (cache invalidation, push) runs in a dry run.

## Tests (test first; DB tests against `TEST_DATABASE_URL`)

**DB, T-603b** (`test/db/control-room.int.test.ts`, `completion.int.test.ts`, `category-rules.int.test.ts`):
pause refuses every start door with QN403 `paused` (scan, code, NFC, kiosk, location, submit, quiz start, vote,
stamp, meet, hand award); resume reopens; a submission sent before the pause is approved and paid; a quiz started
before finishes and pays; replay of a paid key while paused answers `replayed`; same state twice writes no audit row.
Skip: the next in-order step opens; category done and its bonus without the skipped step; unlock-after category
opens; unskip re-holds a player who skipped past it; skip on an any-order quest, flash or live poll → SP422; moving the
quest or switching the category to any order clears the skip. Wipe at go-live clears both. Editors and owners may;
an operator or player may not (AN404).
**DB, T-604** (`test/db/live-edits.int.test.ts`): +50 past for 40 players (FR-411's example), −30, mixed future then
past converges, boosted completion targets `points × multiplier`, removed completion untouched, clamp at score and
balance counted, editor over `award_cap_each` → AC409 (owner passes), ineligible type → PX422, not live → AS409,
results final → RF409, key replay with counts, same key other points → AK422, a run with a new key writes nothing.
Delete keep: scores unchanged, quest gone from staff reads, still named in history. Delete remove: one removal per
kept quest and first-bonus entry, folds adjusts, XP reversed, reserved item cancelled; `remove_award` of an adjusted
entry takes entry plus adjusts. **Invariant test** (seeded random sequence of scans, future and past edits, removals):
every kept completion's net equals `points × multiplier` after the last past edit, unless clamped.
**Races (two connections, both fired at once):** scan in flight vs pause (scan pays, next refused, its retry
replays); scan vs skip of the step before it; scan holding KEY SHARE vs past edit (the edit waits and adjusts it);
past edit holding FOR UPDATE vs scan (the scan pays the new points); two editors' past edits (both commit, invariant
holds); past edit vs `remove_award` and vs `grant_manual_award` on the same quest (no 40P01, invariant holds);
delete-remove vs scan (no completion after the delete); delete-remove retried (no second removal). A bounded stress
run (scans and edits on one quest for 10 seconds) asserts zero deadlocks.
**Outsider** (`test/security/outsider.int.test.ts`): the public role cannot execute `set_quest_paused`,
`set_step_skipped`, `apply_past_points`, `quest_points_impact`, `delete_played_quest`, `write_removal`; cannot read
`quest_points_change`, `change_set`, `change_set_item`. **Cross-tenant** (`cross-tenant.int.test.ts`): an editor of
organizer A cannot pause, skip, past-edit, impact-read, delete or change-set a quest of organizer B (AN404 or 404);
an editor limited to event X cannot act on event Y. Update the community gate map and `rehearsal.int.test.ts` lists.
**HTTP:** each status and body above, 409 `apply_required` / `points_choice_required`, the 24-hour replay of a
`paused` refusal. **Web and app:** room badges and dialogs, editor and delete screens, change-set page; app paused
card and scan answer; `npx expo export` once.

**Mutation globs (tier A only, one run, `!**/*.test.ts`):** `apps/api/src/quests/live-edits.ts` (new: apply, impact
and delete choices, refusal mapping), `apps/api/src/quests/quests.ts`, `apps/api/src/control/pause.ts` (new),
`apps/api/src/home/home.ts`. The SQL is covered by the DB tests above, not by Stryker. Change sets are not mutated.

## Risks

- Restating `record_completion`, `remove_award`, `grant_manual_award`, `quest_open_check`, the category rules,
  `member_event_quests`, `wipe_rehearsal` and `control_done` copies large bodies: diff each against its latest version
  (named above) and change only the stated lines; the existing suites run unchanged.
- KEY SHARE at the first quest read adds a row lock per scan; the ledger FK already takes the same lock later in the
  same transaction, so the cost is moving it earlier, not adding it. A past edit on a quest with thousands of
  completions holds FOR UPDATE for its writes: scans of that one quest wait. Time it with 5,000 completions; if it is
  over 3 seconds, raise it in review (cap or batch).
- A missed staff read shows a deleted quest (the commit's grep table is the guard); a change-set dry run must leak
  no outside effect (one test asserts no audit row, Redis key or push after it).

## Open questions (Proposed; recorded as Q-353 to Q-355 when built)

**Q-353 - T-603b pause and skip: what is decided without Dhia.** Recommended (developer): (1) a pause stops every new
start, hand awards included; work already started (pending submissions, open quiz attempts) finishes and pays;
(2) the pause's commit is the cut-off for scans in flight; (3) a paused in-order step still holds later steps (skip
it to free them); (4) skip pays nobody, the step stays completable, unskip is allowed and its screen counts who is held
again; (5) the go-live wipe clears both. **Open:** should skipping pay the step's points to those who moved on? No.
**Q-354 - T-604 live points edits: what is decided without Dhia.** Recommended: (1) "also past" makes every kept
completion worth the new points times its boost (absolute target, compensating `adjust` entries, never an edit);
(2) only for types paid quest points per completion (not submission, quiz, passport, meet, game); (3) lowering clamps
at zero with no reserved-prize refusal; (4) an editor's past raise stays within the per-award cap; (5) delete with
"remove" also takes the quest's first-N bonus and folded adjusts; category bonuses and streak days stay; (6) a
category holding played quests still cannot be deleted. **Open:** should past edits reach submission and quiz quests?
**Q-355 - Change sets.** Recommended: API-first creation, a dashboard review page, per-item transactions with stale
detection, quests, categories and rewards only. **Open:** should the dashboard itself offer "save as a change set"?
