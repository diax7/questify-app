# T-612: prize reservation On or Off, and the one-step desk sale (plan)

Tier A (CLAUDE.md "Three tiers": redemptions and the points ledger). Plan, plan review, build, one code review, then
mutation. Delivers FR-1834. Depends on T-202 (reserve, 0024/0025), T-206 (the desk, 0029/0031/0032), T-602 (the shop
switch, `feature_enabled(ev, 'shop')`), T-602c (`feature_off` in the doors, 0094), T-602d (held prizes, no migration).
One migration, **0107** (staging runs through 0105; 0106 is pushed, so both are frozen). Proposed at build time:
**ADR-397** and **Q-364** (texts at the end).

## What exists and is reused (nothing new writes points another way)

- `reserve_reward` (latest 0094) is the only shop spend: locks enrollment (FOR NO KEY UPDATE), then reward (FOR UPDATE),
  then a free `reward_code` (digital); inserts the redemption, moves `units_out`, writes one `redeem` ledger row (actor
  `player`, `balance_delta = -price`, `score_delta = -price` only while `spend_lowers_rank` and `play_phase` is not
  NULL, ADR-135), and marks `ranking_repair`. A key that already succeeded replays before any gate (T-602c).
- The handover (`finish_handover`, 0032): assignment (FOR SHARE via `desk_operator`) -> enrollment -> reward ->
  redemption; writes the `handover` ledger row (0/0, actor `operator`, `operator_id`), one per redemption
  (`ledger_entry_handover_key`). The player confirms in their own app (ADR-137, Accepted by Dhia: "he cannot just get
  the prize with just showing the QR, he must redeem it or accept it from his account").
- `desk_lookup` (0047) takes the API-verified My QR pair (player id, `my_qr_version`); OM422 for an earlier code.
- Triggers do the rest on a ledger insert (rehearsal stamp, `apply_ledger_entry` caches, `ledger_operator_play_guard`,
  append-only); `alert_on_reward_low` follows `units_out`; `redemption_event_open` refuses archived events.

## 1. The switch

- **Stored:** `event.reservation_on boolean NOT NULL DEFAULT true` (0107), beside `handover_confirm`. Not a feature
  registry key: it is a mode of the shop, not a feature, and a platform-wide Off would make no sense (ADR-397).
  `GRANT UPDATE (reservation_on) ON event TO questify_app` (column grant only, as 0029 did).
- **Who flips it:** owner or editor (FR-1834), through the existing shop settings door `PUT …/events/{id}/shop`
  (`reservation: boolean` in `shopBody`; `ShopSettings.reservation`; `writeShop` sets the column; audited in the
  existing `event.shop.update` diff). Any status but archived (`withRewardEvent`). Turning it Off needs the desk's
  place (422 `desk_needed`, the existing kind), since every sale is a handover at the desk.
- **The shop On or Off** stays T-602's `feature_enabled(ev, 'shop')`; nothing new. With the shop Off there is neither
  a reservation nor a sale (both refuse RS403 `feature_off`).
- **Reservations already made when it turns Off stand**: Collect, desk offer and Accept, Handed over, Cancel and expiry
  run as today (as T-602c did for the shop switch); the desk lookup still lists them, and T-602d's "Prizes to collect"
  card still shows them. The dashboard's Off confirmation says how many are held, from T-602d's team read
  `GET …/events/{id}/held-prizes` (no new read).
- **What Off changes:** `reserve_reward` refuses an `item` or `experience` reward with RS403 DETAIL `reservation_off`
  (after the shop `feature_off` check, before the window), rehearsal accounts included. **Digital rewards are not
  reservations** (delivered at once, code in the app; the desk cannot hand over a code): they stay in the app. A key
  that already succeeded still replays its first answer.
- **What On changes:** the sale doors refuse with DS409 DETAIL `reservation_on`. One mode at a time (ADR-397).
- **A flip in flight:** like every switch door since T-602c, a door reads the event once, unlocked; the switch writer
  (`lockBuilderEvent`) and the door order on commit. A sale whose accept read Off before the flip committed completes
  whole (the player consented to that price); one that reads On is refused with nothing written. A flip between offer
  and accept (the realistic case, up to 2 minutes) is refused at accept. No event row lock is added (no new lock order).

## 2. The desk sale: one scan, the player's Pay on their phone, one transaction

**Flow.** The operator scans My QR (today's lookup). When the header says reservation Off and the shop on, the desk
shows "Sell from the shop": the event's `item`/`experience` rewards for that player. The operator picks one and taps
Offer. The player, already showing My QR, sees "The prize desk offers Hoodie for 300 points" with Pay and Not now
(balance after the payment shown). Pay is the one step: the price leaves the balance and the item is handed over in
one transaction; the desk's waiting screen turns to "Paid and handed over". Consent is the player's own tap in their
own signed-in app (ADR-137), never the operator's tap alone; FR-1834's "when they confirm" is read as the player's
Pay (Q-364 a). The event's `handover_confirm` does not apply: there is no reservation to Collect.

**Data (0107).** `desk_sale` (an offer, never points):
`id uuid PK DEFAULT gen_random_uuid()`, `organizer_id`, `event_id`, `enrollment_id`, `reward_id` (all NOT NULL),
`operator_id uuid NOT NULL REFERENCES player (id)`, `price integer NOT NULL CHECK (price >= 1)` (the price shown),
`idempotency_key text NOT NULL CHECK (length BETWEEN 1 AND 255)`, `status text NOT NULL DEFAULT 'offered' CHECK (status
IN ('offered','accepted','declined','withdrawn','refused'))`, `refusal text` (an SQLSTATE plus `:detail`, at most 40),
`offer_until timestamptz NOT NULL`, `redemption_id uuid UNIQUE REFERENCES redemption (id) ON DELETE RESTRICT`,
`created_at`, `ended_at`; `UNIQUE (operator_id, idempotency_key)`; `CHECK ((status = 'accepted') = (redemption_id IS
NOT NULL))`, `CHECK ((status = 'refused') = (refusal IS NOT NULL))`, `CHECK ((status = 'offered') = (ended_at IS
NULL))`; composite FKs `(event_id, organizer_id) -> event`, `(enrollment_id, event_id) -> enrollment`, `(reward_id,
event_id) -> reward`, as `redemption` has. `CREATE INDEX desk_sale_open ON desk_sale (enrollment_id) WHERE status =
'offered'`; RLS enabled and forced, **no
policy and no grant**: the definers below are the only doors. An expired offer keeps `status = 'offered'` with
`offer_until` past; readers say `expired` (no tick).

**The shared spend body.** 0107 moves `reserve_reward`'s gates and writes into one internal function, no EXECUTE for
any role: `spend_reward(enrolled enrollment, item reward, ev event, request_key text, at_desk uuid) RETURNS redemption`.
The caller holds the enrollment and reward locks. It runs, in today's order: enrollment active (RA403), redeem paused
(RP403), reward active and in audience (RN404), community lock (RW403), shop `feature_off` (RS403), `reservation_off`
when `at_desk IS NULL` and the kind is a desk kind, `reservation_on` (DS409) when `at_desk` is set, shop window
(rehearsal open), desk (reserve: `desk_finished` and the pickup start as today; desk sale: `desk_state` must be `open`
unless rehearsing, RS403 `desk_closed`), opens/closes (RW403), stock (RO409), per-player limit (RL409, counting every
redemption not cancelled or expired, so handed-over sales count), balance (RB422), the digital code (reserve only),
spend rank (ADR-135). Then the one INSERT: with `at_desk` NULL exactly today's row; with `at_desk` set
`status = 'handed_over'`, `ended_at = now()`, `ended_by = 'handover'`, `operator_id = at_desk`, `reserved_until =
now()` (`redemption_delivered_shape` needs it non-null unless delivered). Then `units_out + 1`, the `redeem` row (actor
`player`: the player paid; `ledger_entry_operator_quests_only` forbids an operator `redeem`), `ranking_repair` when the
score moved, and with `at_desk` set the `handover` row (0/0, actor `operator`, `operator_id = at_desk`). The two rows
are the same rows a reserve plus a handover write today, so every reader counts a sale as a handed-over reservation.
`reserve_reward` becomes: caller check, wipe, enrollment lock, replay, reward lock, `spend_reward(…, NULL)`, answer.
Its answers and refusal order stay byte-identical: every existing reward, desk and switch suite passes unchanged.
Also: `reserve_reward` refuses a key starting with `desk:` (RK422), and `KEY_FORMAT` in `conventions.ts` adds `desk:`
to its reserved prefixes, so a client can never take a sale's redemption key.

**Doors (all SECURITY DEFINER, `search_path = public, pg_temp`, REVOKE ALL FROM PUBLIC, EXECUTE to questify_app only;
`ensure_rehearsal_wiped` first; each ≤100 lines):**

1. `desk_sale_rewards(target_event uuid, scanned uuid, scanned_version integer)` -> rows `reward_id, kind, name_ar,
   name_en, price, stock_left, can text` (`ok`, `too_few_points`, `limit`, `sold_out`, `not_open`): the scanned
   player's offerable rewards (active, desk kinds, in audience, not community-locked), ordered as the shop. Same
   operator and code checks as `desk_lookup` (OP403, OA403, OY409, QC404, OM422); answers no rows when reservation is
   On or the shop is off. The player's balance number is not shown to the desk, only `can`.
2. `desk_offer_sale(target_event uuid, scanned uuid, scanned_version integer, target_reward uuid, request_key text)`
   -> `sale_id, status, price, offer_until, replayed`. Locks: assignment (FOR SHARE, `desk_operator`) -> enrollment
   (FOR NO KEY UPDATE). Replay first: a row with `(operator, request_key)` answers itself (RK422 when it names another
   player or reward). Then: code checks as above, OY409 self, enrollment active (RA403), `desk_hold` (RH409, as
   handovers), reward in this event and a desk kind (RN404; DS422 `not_desk_reward` for digital), reservation Off
   (DS409 `reservation_on`), shop on (RS403 `feature_off`), desk open unless rehearsing (RS403 `desk_closed`). A live
   offer for this player: by another operator, DS409 `desk_busy`; by this operator for the same reward, answered as a
   replay; for another reward, ended `withdrawn` and replaced (the operator changed the pick). Inserts with `price =
   item.price`, `offer_until = now() + 2 minutes` (the desk offer's window, 0031). Writes no points and no stock.
3. `accept_desk_sale(target uuid)` (player) -> `status, redemption_id, balance, refusal, replayed`. Reads the sale
   unlocked joined to the caller's enrollment (RN404 when not theirs; `insufficient_privilege` without a player). Locks
   in the one order: the offering operator's assignment (FOR SHARE) -> enrollment (FOR NO KEY UPDATE) -> reward (FOR
   UPDATE) -> the sale (FOR UPDATE). `accepted` answers the same redemption, `replayed = true`. Not `offered`, or
   `offer_until <= now()`: DS410 (detail `declined`, `withdrawn`, `refused`, `expired`). Then, inside one
   `BEGIN … EXCEPTION` block (a subtransaction that undoes only its own writes): the operator still holds `hand_over`
   (OP403/OA403 are answered to the player as DS410 `withdrawn`), OY409, `desk_hold` (RH409), `item.price =
   sale.price` (DS409 `price_changed`: never a price the player did not see), then `spend_reward(enrolled, item, ev,
   'desk:' || sale.id, sale.operator_id)`, then the sale `accepted` with its `redemption_id`. A refusal caught there
   ends the sale `refused` with its code and answers `status = 'refused'`; the API turns it into the same HTTP error
   the reserve route gives (sold out 409, too few points 422, …) after the commit, so the desk sees why.
4. `decline_desk_sale(target uuid)` (player) and `withdraw_desk_sale(target uuid)` (the offering operator, or any
   operator with `hand_over` in the event): lock the sale only, `offered` -> `declined`/`withdrawn`; false when it had
   already ended. No points.
5. `desk_sale_state(target uuid)` (operator, the desk's 2 s poll, `cache-control: no-store`): status, `expired`
   derived, refusal code. `my_desk_sales()` (player): the caller's live offers in every event (sale id, event, reward
   names, price, `offer_until`, balance after). `operator_desk` (latest 0032) and `event_shop` (latest 0032) gain
   `reservation_on` and the shop switch (`shop_on`); both return types change, so DROP and CREATE with every grant.
   `my_desk_watch` (0029) is restated: also true while the caller is active in an event with reservation Off, the shop
   on and its window open, so My QR polls for an offer.

**Idempotency, exactly.** Offer: the route is `idempotent` (the HTTP store replays the same key), and the database
backs it with `UNIQUE (operator_id, idempotency_key)`; a double tap that sends two keys meets the live-offer rule
(same reward answers the first). Two operators scanning the same player: the second offer gets DS409 `desk_busy`
(both lock the enrollment first, so they serialize). Accept: the route is `idempotent`; the database truth is the sale
row under FOR UPDATE (`accepted` answers again), backed by `desk_sale.redemption_id UNIQUE`, the redemption's
`UNIQUE (enrollment_id, idempotency_key)` with the server-made key `desk:<sale id>`, and `ledger_entry_redeem_key` and
`ledger_entry_handover_key` (one `redeem`, one `handover` per redemption).

**Stock and limits, atomically.** Nothing is held by an offer. Every gate runs again in `accept_desk_sale` under the
enrollment and reward locks, so two desks selling the last unit to two players serialize on the reward row: one is
handed over, the other is refused RO409 with nothing written. The per-player limit and the balance are checked under
the enrollment lock that `reserve_reward` also takes, so an in-app digital purchase and a desk sale cannot both spend
the same points.

**Lock order.** Assignment (FOR SHARE) -> enrollment -> reward (-> reward_code, reserve only) -> desk_sale ->
redemption, the order of 0029 and 0032 with the sale between reward and redemption. The sale doors name no quest and
take no event or quest lock, so `pin_quest`'s event -> quest -> enrollment order (0098) is never inverted; quest-item
paths (enrollment -> quest_item -> redemption) never meet a sale's locks out of order.

**Other sources.** Rank prizes and quest items are price-0 desk reservations made by results and quests, never sold:
untouched by the switch and absent from "Sell from the shop"; the desk keeps handing them over as today.

## 3. API, app and dashboard

- Routes (`apps/api/src/desk/sale.ts` new, wired in `desk/routes.ts`; refusals added to `REFUSALS` and the HTTP errors
  with Arabic and English text): `POST /v1/operator/desk/sale-rewards` `{eventId, code}`; `POST /v1/operator/desk/sales`
  `{eventId, code, rewardId}` (idempotent); `GET /v1/operator/desk/sales/{saleId}`; `POST …/sales/{saleId}/withdraw`;
  `POST /v1/desk-sales/{saleId}/accept` (idempotent) and `/decline`; `GET /v1/me/desk-items` gains `sales`. A bad or
  earlier code answers `{result: "refused", reason}` as the lookup does. New errors: 403 `reservation_off`, 409
  `reservation_on`, 409 `desk_busy`, 409 `price_changed`, 410 `sale_gone`, 422 `not_desk_reward`.
- Shop read: `GET …/shop` carries `reservation` from `event_shop`; settings `PUT …/shop` takes `reservation`.
- App (player): with reservation Off a desk-kind reward shows "Redeem at the prize desk" with the desk's place and
  hours and no Reserve button (reward card, detail sheet, Home's featured reward); digital unchanged. My QR shows the
  offer card (`DeskItems.tsx`): reward, price, balance after, the shop rules on a first redemption, Pay and Not now,
  the 2-minute countdown; Pay's refusals read as plain sentences. No push (the player is at the desk with My QR open).
- App (operator, `app/operator/[eventId]/(tabs)/desk.tsx`, `src/operator/desk.ts`): after a scan, "Sell from the shop"
  (when the header says reservation Off and shop on), Offer, a waiting state polled every `OFFER_POLL_MS`, Withdraw,
  and the outcome. The scanned token stays in memory for that player's screen only. Name search (FR-1848) does not
  sell: a sale needs My QR (Q-364 c). The wallet pass (T-801, not built) joins through the same verified pair.
- Dashboard (`RewardsPanel.tsx`, `lib/rewards.ts`): a "Prize reservation" switch in the shop settings with help text;
  Off asks first and names the held count (T-602d's read).
- Strings in `packages/shared/src/i18n/messages.ts`, Arabic through `ux-araby`, RTL, Western digits.

## 4. Analytics, exports, rehearsal, erasure

- **Analytics (0102) and exports (0103) change nothing:** a sale is a `handed_over` redemption with one `redeem` row,
  so `redeemed`, `spent`, `lowered` and `earned` (score cache plus each standing redemption's own redeem score) count
  it exactly as a reservation handed over; `unclaimed` counts only `reserved`. Offers that were not paid count nowhere.
  A test proves the analytics numbers for one sale equal those for one reserve plus handover.
- **Rehearsal wipe** (`public.wipe_rehearsal`, latest 0097) is restated with `DELETE FROM desk_sale WHERE enrollment_id
  = ANY (accounts)` before the redemption delete (the FK is RESTRICT) and a `sales` count in its answer only if the
  answer already counts reservations the same way. A rehearsal account buys with the desk closed, as its handover can.
- **Erasure** (`erase_rows`, latest 0091) is restated: `DELETE FROM desk_sale WHERE enrollment_id = ANY (enrolled)`,
  and an erased operator's live offers end `withdrawn`. The sale holds no contact detail.

## 6. Risks, ADR-397 and Q-364

- Risks: the `reserve_reward` refactor touches the hottest spend path (refusal order and answers identical; the reward,
  desk, switch and analytics suites first). The EXCEPTION block catches only the listed business codes; anything else
  re-raises. Restated bodies start from their latest (`erase_rows` 0091, `wipe_rehearsal` 0097, `event_shop` and
  `operator_desk` 0032, `my_desk_watch` 0029), copied whole.
- **ADR-397 (Proposed):** reservation is an event column set by the owner or an editor; Off sells desk kinds at the
  desk after a My QR scan, paid by the player's own Pay through the one spend body (one `redeem`, one `handover`);
  digital stays in the app; On has no sales; reservations made before Off stand. Why: FR-1834 with ADR-137.
- **Q-364 (Proposed):** a. the player confirms each sale on their phone, not the operator's tap alone (ADR-137); b.
  digital stays in the app when Off; c. a sale needs My QR (later the pass), not the name search; d. Off needs the
  desk's place, held reservations stand; e. sales only while Off; f. no push for an offer; g. the desk sees "can buy"
  per reward, not the balance.
