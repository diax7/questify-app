## What is reused
- T-309's pairing, the `device` access kind and `request.device` of kind `screen`, `/{lang}/screen` and `DevicePairing`
  (its 3-second `GET /v1/device` poll). Revoke, 24 hours after the end, 30 idle days and the Devices page already apply.
- `rank_top_ten_ids` and `ranking_on` (0082): the ten best under the board's own rule, one index walk. The screen reads
  them from PostgreSQL, so a score is on screen the moment its transaction commits, with no Redis copy to lag or repair.
- `polls_shown`, `quest_community_lock`, the `quest_vote` counts and `poll_results`' policy; `community_goal.progress` (the
  cached column), `community_on`, `community_close_at` as `member_community` reads them. No aggregate over the ledger.

## Data and doors (0083)
- `big_screen (event_id PK, organizer_id, scenes text[], updated_at, updated_by)`: the picked scenes in display order
  (`ranking`, `community`, `poll`; no repeats; 0 to 3). RLS forced, no grant. No row means all three in that order.
- Team (`award_event_of`): `event_big_screen(event)` and `set_big_screen(event, scenes, request)`, audited
  `big_screen.update`. The ranking scene is not offered while `ranking_on` is false (FR-1612) and a save naming it is
  `BS422 ranking_off`. Routes `GET` and `PUT /v1/organizers/:id/events/:eventId/big-screen`; the PUT needs an owner or editor.
- Screen readers, all `definer`, all `current_device()` of kind `screen` (else `DV401`) and reading only that device's
  event: `screen_ranking()`, `screen_community()`, `screen_poll()`; `device_view()` gains `scenes` and `shut: feature_off`
  (the gated `bigScreen` add-on, `big_screen_on(ev)`) or `event_ended`. Routes: `GET /v1/device/screen/:scene` (device kind
  screen only, 40 a minute per device, `Cache-Control: no-store`); one shared 2-second answer per event and scene bounds the
  database however many screens an event pairs (the poll counts are an aggregate, so they are cached, never per request).
- The poll scene shows the newest sent poll that is open, else the one that closed in the last 15 minutes, only if it is
  public (no audience, category not hidden, not community-locked). Results by its policy (T-306 note): `after_vote` live,
  `at_close` once closed, `never` the vote count only (FR-1613); `at_close` still open also shows the count only.

## Page
- `/{lang}/screen` replaces the paired placeholder: `BigScreen` rotates the picked scenes every 15 seconds (fixed; a
  per-event time is not built until asked), skipping a scene with nothing to show; the open scene re-reads every 3 seconds;
  the scene list rides the existing device poll, so a change on the dashboard or a Revoke reaches the TV in 3 seconds.
  Brand deck style, big type, Arabic and English, RTL, Western digits. Dashboard: a Big screen page (pick, order, save).
- **No server-sent stream (ADR-221 left it here).** Polling at 3 seconds meets FR-632's "within seconds", reuses the cookie
  guard and rate limit so a revoked screen blanks at the next poll, and needs no long-lived connection through the proxy or
  reconnect logic. A stream is built only if a scene needs sub-second updates.
