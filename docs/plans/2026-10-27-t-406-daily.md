### Locks (the T-404 MAJOR-1 lesson)

Under `SET LOCAL lock_timeout = '10s'`, every existing table 0069 touches is locked up front, in live-path order, at the
strongest mode a later statement needs:

1. `xp_entry` **ACCESS EXCLUSIVE**: the new column, its foreign key and the restated shape CHECK. First, because every
   live path that reaches `player_xp` has touched `xp_entry` before it (the sync's unlocked pre-check reads it; a
   completion's `xp_credit` inserts it before `xp_apply` updates `player_xp`), so nothing holding `player_xp` waits on
   the migration while the migration waits on it.
2. `player` **SHARE ROW EXCLUSIVE** (MINOR-4): FK target of `daily_play`. Compatible with the KEY SHARE every live
   path takes on it; it waits only for transactions writing player rows (sign-up, profile edits, `erase_player`). Before
   `player_xp` because sign-up inserts the player before its `player_xp` row; none of these writers touches `xp_entry`
   (checked at build: a `pg_proc` sweep of every writer of `player`). It blocks player writes for the migration's
   length only (well under a second; the upgrade test times it).
3. `player_xp` **SHARE ROW EXCLUSIVE**: FK target of `daily_finish`. Compatible with the row locks (ROW SHARE) the sync
   and `gem_buy` take; it waits only for transactions already updating it, none of which then needs `xp_entry` or
   `player` in a stronger mode without having touched it first (checked at build: `pg_proc` sweep of every writer of
   `player_xp`).

**Never** `ledger_entry`, `enrollment`, `event` (0069 changes nothing on the event side), `platform_setting` (no row is added; every amount and switch defaults when absent),
`questify_streak_day` (only written, never altered) or `audit_log`. The upgrade test below runs 0069 while a sync, a
first-time quest completion, a sign-up and a `gem_buy` are each in flight: each waits and completes, no deadlock, and the lock list
matches the tables the SQL alters or references.

### Periods and cadence (ADR-307, Q-273)

- **The day** is T-405's `questify_day(at)` (04:00 to 04:00 Riyadh), so "the same word for everyone that day" and the
  daily streak share one boundary.
- **The cadence** is per kind (`challenge`, `word`): `daily` or `weekly`. **Weekly periods run Sunday to Saturday** (the
  Saudi week, as the T-405b weekday chips).
- `daily_cadence (kind, from_day date, cadence, set_by, created_at)`, PK (kind, from_day). The cadence of a day is the row
  with the greatest `from_day ≤ day`. Seeded: both kinds `weekly` from `2026-01-04` (a Sunday; ADR-099 "weekly at
  launch"). A change is written for **tomorrow**, as FR-1814 says ("from the next day"). **The cadence door (MAJOR-1,
  MAJOR-2)** first locks the row governing today `FOR UPDATE` (every player door holds that row FOR KEY SHARE while it
  computes its period, below), then computes tomorrow as `questify_day(clock_timestamp()) + 1`, then:
  - the cadence asked for **equals the one governing today**: any pending row for tomorrow is deleted and the answer is
    `{unchanged: true}` (without this, a pending "weekly" row written over a running weekly period would start a new
    period tomorrow keyed on a day with no content, and the challenge would be null until Saturday);
  - otherwise tomorrow's row is inserted, or replaces a pending one.
  A trigger refuses an INSERT, UPDATE or DELETE of any row whose `from_day ≤ questify_day(clock_timestamp())` (the
  owner role included), so a change committing after 04:00 can never land on a day that has come; and because the door
  holds today's governing row, a player door at 04:00 either computes its period before the change exists or waits and
  computes it after the commit, never a mix. Tested crossed, both orders.
- `daily_period(kind, day) RETURNS (starts_on date, ends_on date)` (internal): daily → `(day, day)`; weekly → `starts_on =
  greatest(day's Sunday, the governing row's from_day)`, `ends_on = least(that Sunday + 6, the next row's from_day − 1)`.
  So a switch to daily from Tuesday ends the running week on Monday; a switch to weekly from Wednesday gives a short
  first week (Wednesday to Saturday). Both are pinned by DB tests.
- **Content is keyed by its period's first day** (`starts_on`). Content saved on a day that is not a period start under
  the cadence known when it is saved is refused (`DK422 not_period_start`); content left on a non-start day by a later
  cadence change is never served and is listed as `unused` by the console read (the cadence door's answer counts it);
  an unused row with no plays can be cleared at any time, its day passed or not (MINOR-7: the freeze trigger allows
  exactly that DELETE).

**Letters (ADR-310).** `word_norm(language, text)` (internal, IMMUTABLE): English lower-cases and must match
`^[a-z]{5}$`; Arabic strips the diacritics (U+064B to U+065F, U+0670) and the tatweel (U+0640), folds أ إ آ ٱ to ا, and
must then be five letters of an **explicit 33-letter set** (MINOR-6): ا ب ت ث ج ح خ د ذ ر ز س ش ص ض ط ظ ع غ ف ق ك ل م
ن ه و ي plus ة ى ء ؤ ئ, each its own letter with its own key (not the U+0621 to U+064A range, which also holds أ إ آ,
the tatweel and unused letters). The same sets live in `packages/shared/src/daily/letters.ts` for the app's keyboard; a
DB test reads `word_norm`'s set from the catalog and asserts it equals the shared one. Anything else is refused
(`DW422 letters`/`length` for a guess, `DK422 word` for the console).

**Warnings (FR-1733, FR-1811, FR-1814).** `daily_coverage(kind, language)` (internal): from today's period forward, the
number of consecutive periods with complete content (a challenge with its three questions; a word for that language) and
the days they cover. **Challenge warns** when fewer than 7 days or fewer than 2 periods are covered (7 days for daily,
14 for weekly). **Each language's word warns** when fewer than 2 periods are covered (which includes "no word for
tomorrow" under the daily cadence). A missing current period simply covers 0.

### Rewards (ADR-309, Q-275)

- **Challenge:** every answer is final and answered in order (1, 2, 3). Finishing pays **20 XP plus 10 per right
  answer** (20 to 50; a quest pays 100), whatever the score, so "answer the three questions, then they earn XP"
  (FR-730) holds for every player.
- **Word:** **50 XP when solved**, none when not (FR-1810), flat whatever the number of guesses.
- **Amounts are data:** `platform_setting` key `xp` (the block `quest_xp()` reads) fields `dailyFinishXp`,
  `dailyRightXp`, `dailyWordXp`, falling back per field (the `gem_amounts()` pattern) to the default when absent or
  outside its range. **Ranges sized so the largest payout fits `daily_finish.xp` (MAJOR-3):** `dailyFinishXp` 0–400,
  `dailyRightXp` 0–200 (400 + 3 × 200 = 1000), `dailyWordXp` 0–1000. A DB test sets every field to its maximum and
  finishes both games (1000 XP each, no CHECK failure), and one above the range to see the default used. The console
  editor is T-703/T-709's and must show the same ranges.
- **Paid in the door, not by `questify_sync`:** the finish is the player's own action in its own transaction, never an
  event completion, so paying here adds nothing to the completion path (the reason T-403 and T-405 pay through the
  sync), and paying at once lets the answer carry the XP and `levelUp`
  (`questify_level_up(who, entries)`). Level items follow `xp_apply` as for any XP; **a level crossed pays its level
  gems at the next `questify_sync`** (T-404's retroactive step; the app syncs after a finish), never in the door.
- **Gems never:** no `gem_entry` source; tested by the catalog query T-405 added.
- **The daily streak (FR-731, ADR-297):** a **finished** play keeps the day: the challenge's third answer, or the word's
  solving guess or sixth guess (solved or not: it is play, as T-405 counts repeats). Only while `daily_streak_on()`.
  Under the weekly cadence, finishing the week's challenge keeps only the day it was finished.

### The player doors (granted)

Every door: the caller is an active player (`player FOR KEY SHARE`, else `PI401`; staff sessions never reach player
doors, T-402 pattern); the switch on (`daily_feature_on(kind)`, else `DC404`/`DW404 unavailable`; switched off
mid-play, the play stays unfinished and pays nothing, tested); the content is the current period's
(`questify_day(now())`), else `DC409`/`DW409 period_over`, **checked before anything about the content (a right answer,
marks) is computed or returned** (MINOR-5). **Lock order, every door:** player KEY SHARE → today's governing
`daily_cadence` row FOR KEY SHARE (then the period is computed, in a later statement, so it sees a cadence change that
committed while the door waited) → the content row FOR KEY SHARE (content doors take it FOR UPDATE, which conflicts;
MAJOR-1) → `daily_play` (INSERT … ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING, then SELECT … FOR UPDATE) → append the
move → **on a finish only:** `questify_streak_day` (probe, INSERT … ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING) → `player_xp` FOR UPDATE →
`daily_finish`, `xp_entry (daily)`, `xp_apply`. This keeps T-405's order (the streak row before `player_xp`, as in the
completion trigger), so a door crossing a completion of the same player waits at most on the other's streak row and
never holds anything the other needs; the sync takes player KEY SHARE then `player_xp`, which the door takes last. An
answer that does not finish takes no `player_xp` lock at all.

- `daily_answer_submit(challenge bigint, question uuid, pos smallint, chosen smallint)` → `{answer: {position, choice,
  correct, right}, finished: {correct, xp} | null, levelUp, dailyStreak, replayed}`. `question` is the question the
  player was shown at `pos`; if the locked challenge holds another one there, `DC409 changed` and nothing is written
  (`own_daily` is a lock-free read, so a player may have read content that a save racing 04:00 then replaced; the app
  refetches; MAJOR-1). `pos` must be the next one (`DC409 out_of_order`); an existing `(play, pos)` with the same choice
  replays the stored answer (`replayed: true`, nothing written), with another choice `DC409 answered`; a choice outside
  the options `DC422 choice`.
- `daily_guess_submit(word bigint, try smallint, guess text)` → `{guess: {attempt, guess, marks}, finished: {solved,
  attempts, xp, word} | null, levelUp, dailyStreak, replayed}`. `word` is the id the player was shown; a replaced word
  has a new id, so a stale one answers `DW409 period_over`/`changed` (MAJOR-1). `try` must be the next attempt (`DW409 out_of_order`); an
  existing `(play, try)` with the same normalized guess replays, another `DW409 attempt_taken`; a finished play
  `DW409 finished`; another language's word in the same period `DW409 other_language`; a refused guess (`DW422 length |
  letters | not_a_word`) **uses no attempt** and writes nothing. Marks: the two-pass rule (exact letters first, then
  "elsewhere" only while the word still has an unmatched copy of that letter), so duplicate letters are marked right.
  **The word is returned only in `finished`** (as `display`).
- **A replayed finishing move** (MINOR-7) returns `finished` read from `daily_finish` (and `levelUp: null`, the level
  was shown the first time), so a retry of a lost last answer or last guess still shows the result.
- **What no refusal carries** (MINOR-5): no error MESSAGE, DETAIL or HINT, audit row, log line or replay names a right
  answer the caller has not answered or a word the caller has not finished; `DC409 answered` and `DW409 attempt_taken`
  carry no stored value.
- `own_daily()` (STABLE) → `{challenge: {id, period: {startsOn, endsOn}, sponsor: {ar, en} | null, questions: [{position,
  prompt: {ar, en}, options: [{ar, en}], answered: {choice, correct, right} | null}], finished: {correct, xp} | null} |
  null, word: {period, length: 5, languages: ["ar"?, "en"?], play: {language, wordId, guesses: [{guess, marks}],
  finished: {solved, attempts, xp, word} | null} | null, words: [{language, id}]} | null}`. **Never the right answer of an
  unanswered question, never an unfinished word.** `null` when the switch is off or the period has no content (FR-1733,
  FR-1811: no challenge, no game). A language with no word that period is simply absent from `words`.
- **Natural replay keys instead of an Idempotency-Key:** a retry of a lost answer sends the same (position, choice) or
  (attempt, guess) and gets the stored result; the player cannot be charged twice because nothing is spent, and XP is
  once per play by `daily_finish.play_id UNIQUE`.

### The console doors (granted; `questify_admin()`, else `QY403`)

Each re-reads the admin from the account (0066 pattern), replays by request key as 0066's doors do, and audits
`daily.*` with ids and changed field names only (never a word or an answer). **The 04:00 race (MAJOR-1).** A plain
UPDATE takes FOR NO KEY UPDATE, which does not conflict with a player door's FOR KEY SHARE, so every content door takes
an **explicit `FOR UPDATE`** on the row it changes before it writes (a new row: the day's existing row if any, else the
INSERT's unique key). Then it **checks "has the period started" with `questify_day(clock_timestamp())`**, after the lock,
so a save racing 04:00 either commits before a player door locks the row (the door then reads the saved content) or is
refused (`DK409 started`); a player who read the old content without a lock (`own_daily`) is caught by the question id
or word id in the answer (`changed`). Content doors lock only content rows, never a player, play or XP row.

- `daily_question_save(target uuid, fields jsonb, request uuid)`: **first locks the challenge that schedules the
  question `FOR UPDATE` (if any), then the question row `FOR UPDATE`**, then the clock check: a question in a started
  challenge is refused (`DK409 started`), so a player door holding that challenge FOR KEY SHARE and an edit of one of
  its questions are serialized. `daily_question_retire(target, request)` (the same locks; refused while scheduled:
  `DK409 scheduled`; a used one is already frozen).
- `daily_challenge_set(day date, questions uuid[], sponsor jsonb, request)`: exactly three distinct `ready` questions not
  used by another challenge (`DK409 used {position}`), `day` a future period start (`DK409 started`, `DK422
  not_period_start`), sponsor `{ar, en}` or null; replaces that day's three rows in one statement group.
  `daily_challenge_clear(day, request)` (future only).
- `daily_word_set(language, day, word, request)`: normalized, five letters (`DK422 word`), not already a daily word in
  that language (`DK409 used`), future period start; the row is replaced (delete, insert: a new id), never updated;
  also adds the word to the guess list. `daily_word_clear(language, day, request)`; both clears also take an unused row
  with no plays whatever its day (MINOR-7).
- `word_list_change(language, adds text[], removes text[], request)`: at most 20,000 each per call; answers
  `{added, removed, refused: [first 50 invalid inputs], total}`. A removal never touches stored guesses or daily words.
- `daily_cadence_set(kind, cadence, request)`: as in "Periods and cadence" (today's governing row FOR UPDATE, tomorrow
  from `clock_timestamp()`, the same cadence deletes a pending row); answers `{kind, cadence, from, unused}` or
  `{kind, cadence, unchanged: true, unused}`.
- Reads: `daily_console(from date, to date)` (≤ 120 days): `{status: {challenge: {cadence, next: {cadence, from} | null,
  coveredDays, coveredPeriods, warn}, word: {ar: {…}, en: {…}}}, periods: [{kind, startsOn, endsOn, challenge?:
  {id, questions: [id], sponsor, finishes}, words?: {ar?, en?: {id, word, finishes}}}], unused: [{kind, language?,
  startsOn}]}`; `finishes` is a count (Questify's own report to a sponsor: counts only, never players, ADR-067).
  `daily_question_list(status, before, page)` (with answers: admins write them); `word_list_page(language, prefix,
  page)` and its total.

### The switches (ADR-312, Q-278)

`FEATURES.dailyChallenge` and `FEATURES.dailyWord` = `{gated: false, level: "platform"}` in
`packages/shared/src/config/registry.ts` (on by default), shipped in `/v1/config`, honoured in SQL by
`daily_feature_on(kind)` (the `daily_streak_on()` read). Off: `own_daily` answers that kind as `null`, the doors refuse,
nothing is counted, at once. T-703's switches page offers both; until then `savePlatformSettings` flips them (a route
test drives it). Switching the daily streak off leaves the games playable but writes no streak row (FR-731's second
Given).

## Build notes (2026-10-27, T-406 core)

**The re-review's last items, as built.**
1. `daily_cadence_set` locks every cadence row of its kind `FOR UPDATE`, then reads `clock_timestamp()` once and works
   out today, the governing row and tomorrow from that one read; the freeze trigger re-checks the clock, so a change
   whose read and write straddle 04:00 is refused. Tested: a cadence door that waited on a player door across 04:00
   writes for the new tomorrow and nothing lands on the day that came (`daily-content.int`).
2. Content doors never lock `daily_cadence`: `not_period_start` reads it through `daily_period_start` with no lock (the
   lock-order notes in `docs/rules.md` say so) and a test pins it: a challenge save crossing a cadence door that holds
   every row `FOR UPDATE` does not wait.
3. An unknown word id answers `changed`, a known word of another period `period_over` (and the same for challenges,
   ADR-313). Tested in `daily-word.int` and `test/security/daily-players.int`.
4. The cadence freeze trigger is created after the 2026-01-04 seed. `daily_word` has `CHECK (word_norm(language,
   display) IS NOT DISTINCT FROM word)` (so a display that does not normalize fails the CHECK instead of passing it as
   NULL); `word_guess_list` has the same CHECK on its word.

**Gates.** Deny by default: every table forces RLS with no API or PUBLIC right and every function is revoked from PUBLIC
next to its `CREATE`; only the 3 player doors and 11 console doors are granted (the outsider suite lists all 58 new
functions, so a new one cannot be missed). The lock list is exactly `xp_entry` ACCESS EXCLUSIVE, `player` and
`player_xp` SHARE ROW EXCLUSIVE (`daily-upgrade.int`: the list matches the tables the SQL alters or references; a sync,
a completion, a sign-up and a gem purchase in flight each finish; no writer of `player` touches `xp_entry`; with no
traffic it runs in about 0.1 s). The lock order puts the streak row before `player_xp` (crossed tests with an event
completion, the other game's finish, `questify_sync`, `gem_buy` and `erase_player`, both orders). XP is paid once per
play (`daily_finish.play_id` UNIQUE; replays read the finish). The secrecy sweep lists the granted doors that touch the
daily tables (so a new door fails it) and searches results, refusals, audit rows and replays.

**Timing (ADR-303, by hand).** `test/perf/streak-timing.int` gained two kinds: `daily` (the streak burst while 300 daily
finishes of the same players run at once on a third pool) and `load` (the same with 300 players outside the event).
Median p95 of six rounds, two runs: plain 70.0 / 69.6 ms; streak work +15.4 / +20.3 ms; `daily` 144.9 / 142.0 ms, of
which the shared streak row is +10.6 ms over `load` and the 300 extra concurrent transactions about +62 ms; every
streak completion paid one bonus and every finish paid. Inside NFR-002's 1 s; the reading of the budget is ADR-313 item
8 / Q-282.

**Tests.** The DB suites cannot move the clock, so `test/support/daily.ts` replaces this database's `questify_day` with a
copy that adds a shift from `test_clock`; content is set while the clock stands before its day, then the clock moves
onto it (ADR-313). The 04:00 races cross a player door and a content or cadence door both ways, moving the clock between
the first lock and the commit.

**Build choices:** Proposed ADR-313 / Q-282.
