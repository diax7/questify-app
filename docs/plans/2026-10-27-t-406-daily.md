### Locks (the T-404 MAJOR-1 lesson)

Under `SET LOCAL lock_timeout = '10s'`, every existing table 0069 touches is locked up front, in live-path order, at the
strongest mode a later statement needs:

1. `xp_entry` **ACCESS EXCLUSIVE**: the new column, its foreign key and the restated shape CHECK. First, because every
   live path that reaches `player_xp` has touched `xp_entry` before it (the sync's unlocked pre-check reads it; a
   completion's `xp_credit` inserts it before `xp_apply` updates `player_xp`), so nothing holding `player_xp` waits on
   the migration while the migration waits on it.
2. `player_xp` **SHARE ROW EXCLUSIVE**: FK target of `daily_finish`. Compatible with the row locks (ROW SHARE) the sync
   and `gem_buy` take; it waits only for transactions already updating it, none of which then needs `xp_entry` without
   having touched it first (checked at build: `pg_proc` sweep of every writer of `player_xp`).

**Never** `ledger_entry`, `enrollment`, `event` (0069 changes nothing on the event side), `player` (no new foreign key
points at it: see `daily_play`), `platform_setting` (no row is added; every amount and switch defaults when absent),
`questify_streak_day` (only written, never altered) or `audit_log`. The upgrade test below runs 0069 while a sync, a
first-time quest completion and a `gem_buy` are each in flight: each waits and completes, no deadlock, and the lock list
matches the tables the SQL alters or references.

### Periods and cadence (ADR-307, Q-273)

- **The day** is T-405's `questify_day(at)` (04:00 to 04:00 Riyadh), so "the same word for everyone that day" and the
  daily streak share one boundary.
- **The cadence** is per kind (`challenge`, `word`): `daily` or `weekly`. **Weekly periods run Sunday to Saturday** (the
  Saudi week, as the T-405b weekday chips).
- `daily_cadence (kind, from_day date, cadence, set_by, created_at)`, PK (kind, from_day). The cadence of a day is the row
  with the greatest `from_day ≤ day`. Seeded: both kinds `weekly` from `2026-01-04` (a Sunday; ADR-099 "weekly at
  launch"). A change is written for **tomorrow** (`questify_day(now()) + 1`), as FR-1814 says ("from the next day");
  a second change on the same day replaces tomorrow's row; rows whose `from_day` has come are frozen (trigger refuses
  UPDATE and DELETE; the owner role included).
- `daily_period(kind, day) RETURNS (starts_on date, ends_on date)` (internal): daily → `(day, day)`; weekly → `starts_on =
  greatest(day's Sunday, the governing row's from_day)`, `ends_on = least(that Sunday + 6, the next row's from_day − 1)`.
  So a switch to daily from Tuesday ends the running week on Monday; a switch to weekly from Wednesday gives a short
  first week (Wednesday to Saturday). Both are pinned by DB tests.
- **Content is keyed by its period's first day** (`starts_on`). Content saved on a day that is not a period start under
  the cadence known when it is saved is refused (`DK422 not_period_start`); content left on a non-start day by a later
  cadence change is never served and is listed as `unused` by the console read (the cadence door's answer counts it).

**Letters (ADR-310).** `word_norm(language, text)` (internal, IMMUTABLE): English lower-cases and must match
`^[a-z]{5}$`; Arabic strips the diacritics (U+064B to U+065F, U+0670) and the tatweel (U+0640), folds أ إ آ ٱ to ا, and
must then be five letters of U+0621 to U+064A; ة, ى, ء, ؤ and ئ stay their own letters (the app's keyboard has a key for
each). Anything else is refused (`DW422 letters`/`length` for a guess, `DK422 word` for the console).

**Warnings (FR-1733, FR-1811, FR-1814).** `daily_coverage(kind, language)` (internal): from today's period forward, the
number of consecutive periods with complete content (a challenge with its three questions; a word for that language) and
the days they cover. **Challenge warns** when fewer than 7 days or fewer than 2 periods are covered (7 days for daily,
14 for weekly). **Each language's word warns** when fewer than 2 periods are covered (which includes "no word for
tomorrow" under the daily cadence). A missing current period simply covers 0.

### Rewards (ADR-309, Q-275)

- **Challenge:** every answer is final and answered in order (1, 2, 3). Finishing pays **20 XP plus 10 per right
  answer** (20 to 50; a quest pays 100), whatever the score, so "answer the three questions, then they earn XP"
  (FR-730) holds for every player.
- **Word:** **50 XP when solved**, none when not (FR-1810), flat whatever the number of guesses.
- **Amounts are data:** `platform_setting` key `xp` (the block `quest_xp()` reads) fields `dailyFinishXp`,
  `dailyRightXp`, `dailyWordXp`, each 0 to 1000, falling back per field (the `gem_amounts()` pattern). The console
  editor is T-703/T-709's.
- **Paid in the door, not by `questify_sync`:** the finish is the player's own action in its own transaction, never an
  event completion, so paying here adds nothing to the completion path (the reason T-403 and T-405 pay through the
  sync), and paying at once lets the answer carry the XP and `levelUp`
  (`questify_level_up(who, entries)`). Level items follow `xp_apply` as for any XP; **a level crossed pays its level
  gems at the next `questify_sync`** (T-404's retroactive step; the app syncs after a finish), never in the door.
- **Gems never:** no `gem_entry` source; tested by the catalog query T-405 added.
- **The daily streak (FR-731, ADR-297):** a **finished** play keeps the day: the challenge's third answer, or the word's
  solving guess or sixth guess (solved or not: it is play, as T-405 counts repeats). Only while `daily_streak_on()`.
  Under the weekly cadence, finishing the week's challenge keeps only the day it was finished.

### The player doors (granted)

Every door: the caller is an active player (`player FOR KEY SHARE`, else `PI401`; staff sessions never reach player
doors, T-402 pattern); the switch on (`daily_feature_on(kind)`, else `DC404`/`DW404 unavailable`); the content is the
current period's (`questify_day(now())`), else `DC409`/`DW409 period_over`. **Lock order, every door:** player KEY SHARE
→ the content row FOR KEY SHARE → `daily_play` (INSERT … ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING, then SELECT … FOR UPDATE) → append the
move → **on a finish only:** `questify_streak_day` (probe, INSERT … ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING) → `player_xp` FOR UPDATE →
`daily_finish`, `xp_entry (daily)`, `xp_apply`. This keeps T-405's order (the streak row before `player_xp`, as in the
completion trigger), so a door crossing a completion of the same player waits at most on the other's streak row and
never holds anything the other needs; the sync takes player KEY SHARE then `player_xp`, which the door takes last. An
answer that does not finish takes no `player_xp` lock at all.

- `daily_answer_submit(challenge bigint, pos smallint, chosen smallint)` → `{answer: {position, choice, correct,
  right}, finished: {correct, xp} | null, levelUp, dailyStreak, replayed}`. `pos` must be the next one (`DC409
  out_of_order`); an existing `(play, pos)` with the same choice replays the stored answer (`replayed: true`, nothing
  written), with another choice `DC409 answered`; a choice outside the options `DC422 choice`.
- `daily_guess_submit(word bigint, try smallint, guess text)` → `{guess: {attempt, guess, marks}, finished: {solved,
  attempts, xp, word} | null, levelUp, dailyStreak, replayed}`. `try` must be the next attempt (`DW409 out_of_order`); an
  existing `(play, try)` with the same normalized guess replays, another `DW409 attempt_taken`; a finished play
  `DW409 finished`; another language's word in the same period `DW409 other_language`; a refused guess (`DW422 length |
  letters | not_a_word`) **uses no attempt** and writes nothing. Marks: the two-pass rule (exact letters first, then
  "elsewhere" only while the word still has an unmatched copy of that letter), so duplicate letters are marked right.
  **The word is returned only in `finished`.**
- `own_daily()` (STABLE) → `{challenge: {id, period: {startsOn, endsOn}, sponsor: {ar, en} | null, questions: [{position,
  prompt: {ar, en}, options: [{ar, en}], answered: {choice, correct, right} | null}], finished: {correct, xp} | null} |
  null, word: {period, length: 5, languages: ["ar"?, "en"?], play: {language, wordId, guesses: [{guess, marks}],
  finished: {solved, attempts, xp, word} | null} | null, words: [{language, id}]} | null}`. **Never the right answer of an
  unanswered question, never an unfinished word.** `null` when the switch is off or the period has no content (FR-1733,
  FR-1811: no challenge, no game). A language with no word that period is simply absent from `words`.
- **Natural replay keys instead of an Idempotency-Key:** a retry of a lost answer sends the same (position, choice) or
  (attempt, guess) and gets the stored result; the player cannot be charged twice because nothing is spent, and XP is
  once per play by `daily_finish.play_id UNIQUE`.

### The console doors (granted; `questify_admin()`, else `QY403`)

Each re-reads the admin from the account (0066 pattern), replays by request key as 0066's doors do, and audits
`daily.*` with ids and changed field names only (never a word or an answer). Content doors lock only their own rows and
**check "has the period started" with `questify_day(clock_timestamp())` after taking the row lock**, so a save racing
04:00 either lands before any player read the period (the player door's KEY SHARE waited for it) or is refused
(`DK409 started`).

- `daily_question_save(target uuid, fields jsonb, request uuid)`, `daily_question_retire(target, request)` (refused while
  scheduled: `DK409 scheduled`; a used one is already frozen).
- `daily_challenge_set(day date, questions uuid[], sponsor jsonb, request)`: exactly three distinct `ready` questions not
  used by another challenge (`DK409 used {position}`), `day` a future period start (`DK409 started`, `DK422
  not_period_start`), sponsor `{ar, en}` or null; replaces that day's three rows in one statement group.
  `daily_challenge_clear(day, request)` (future only).
- `daily_word_set(language, day, word, request)`: normalized, five letters (`DK422 word`), not already a daily word in
  that language (`DK409 used`), future period start; also adds the word to the guess list. `daily_word_clear(language,
  day, request)`.
- `word_list_change(language, adds text[], removes text[], request)`: at most 20,000 each per call; answers
  `{added, removed, refused: [first 50 invalid inputs], total}`. A removal never touches stored guesses or daily words.
- `daily_cadence_set(kind, cadence, request)`: writes tomorrow's row; answers `{kind, cadence, from, unused}`.
- Reads: `daily_console(from date, to date)` (≤ 120 days): `{status: {challenge: {cadence, next: {cadence, from} | null,
  coveredDays, coveredPeriods, warn}, word: {ar: {…}, en: {…}}}, periods: [{kind, startsOn, endsOn, challenge?:
  {id, questions: [id], sponsor, finishes}, words?: {ar?, en?: {id, word, finishes}}}], unused: [{kind, language?,
  startsOn}]}`; `finishes` is a count (Questify's own report to a sponsor: counts only, never players, ADR-067).
  `daily_question_list(status, before, page)` (with answers: admins write them); `word_list_page(language, prefix,
  page)` and its total.

- **Challenge (FR-730):** three answers in order pay 20 + 10 × right once; 0 right still pays 20; a second play of the
  same period is impossible (replay or `answered`); out of order refused; a choice outside the options refused; the
  next period gives a new challenge; a weekly challenge is one play for the week; `own_daily` never holds an
  unanswered right answer (the JSON is searched for it).
- **Word (FR-1810):** solved in four → `solved`, 4 attempts, 50 XP, streak row; six misses → the word revealed, no XP,
  no `xp_entry`, streak row; marks on a table of duplicate-letter cases (Arabic and English) and on 500 random pairs
  against a reference implementation in the test; a guess not in the list, of the wrong length or with other letters
  uses no attempt; the period's own word is always a valid guess; normalization (أ → ا, diacritics and tatweel
  stripped); one game per period across languages (`other_language`); replay of an attempt; `own_daily` never holds an
  unfinished word.
- **Streak (FR-731):** a finish with no event play keeps the day (`daily_streak_length` 2 after yesterday's completion);
  a finish on a day already counted writes nothing new; switch off → no row, the game still pays XP; a partial play
  counts nothing.
- **Content (FR-732, FR-1733, FR-1811, FR-1814):** sponsor shown for its period only; no challenge → `challenge: null`,
  and the streak still counts by an event completion; no word in a language → that language absent, none at all →
  `word: null`; the warnings at 6 vs 7 days (daily) and 1 vs 2 weeks (weekly), per word language; a question used once;
  a started period, its questions and its word refuse every change (doors and the owner role); the 04:00 race (an admin
  save holding the row across the boundary is refused by the clock check; a player door waiting on it reads the saved
  content); `not_period_start`; `unused` after a cadence change; the guess list's add/remove counts and refusals.
- **XP and gems:** `xp_entry (daily)` shape CHECK refuses forged rows; XP invariant (`player_xp.xp = sum(delta)`) after
  each file; a level crossed by daily XP grants the level item at once and its gems at the next sync (through the level
  source); the catalog query: `gem_entry` writers unchanged and no daily source.
- **Concurrency (crossed, both orders):** two answers of one play at once (one written, the other replays or is
  refused); the challenge's last answer and the word's last guess of one player at once (no deadlock, one streak row,
  both paid); a finish crossing the same player's first event completion of the day (both write or skip the streak row,
  no deadlock); a finish crossing `questify_sync` and `gem_buy`; `erase_player` crossing a finish (no deadlock, nothing
  written after the erasure commits); a console save crossing a player read of the same period.
- **Append-only:** UPDATE/DELETE/TRUNCATE refused on the four play tables and on frozen content.
- **Migration** (`daily-upgrade.int`, the `gems-upgrade.int` style): 0069 applied while a sync, a first-time quest
  completion and a `gem_buy` are in flight: each waits and completes; the lock list matches the tables the SQL alters
  or references.

**Security** (`apps/api/test/security/*`): **outsider:** the ten new tables closed to `anon`, `PUBLIC` and the API role;
every new function refused to `anon` and `PUBLIC`; the internals (`word_norm`, `daily_period`, `daily_coverage`,
`daily_feature_on`, the marks function, the freeze triggers) not granted. **Cross-player** (Questify-level data, so the
tenant boundary is the player): B's `own_daily` shows nothing of A's play; B cannot answer into A's play (doors take
the caller only); no granted read returns `daily_word.word` before that caller finished it, or `daily_question.answer`
before that caller answered it (a sweep over every granted function a player can call, searching the output).
**Staff and organizers:** an organizer's staff session gets `QY403` from every console door and cannot enter player
doors; a player session gets `QY403` from console doors. The rehearsal guard list, the community gate map and the
completeness maps gain the new objects. **Route/unit:** shapes, auth, refusal mapping by SQLSTATE and DETAIL, rate
limits, the registry keys, the shared schemas and letter sets.

**Timing (by hand, recorded in STATE; ADR-303):** re-time `apps/api/test/perf/streak-timing.int.test.ts` with a fourth
kind: the same 300 first-of-the-day completions while a third pool finishes 300 daily challenges of the same players
(every finish writes the streak row first, so half the pairs collide on it). Target: the streak burst's p95 stays within
the about 20 ms budget over the plain one, within NFR-002's 1 s.

## Proposed decisions and questions

- **ADR-306 / Q-272** the split: T-406 core (migration 0069, API, shared), T-406b app, T-406c console.
- **ADR-307 / Q-273** periods: the Questify day; weekly periods Sunday to Saturday; a cadence per kind, changed from the
  next day, weekly at launch; content keyed by its period's first day.
- **ADR-308 / Q-274** content: a two-language question bank (2–4 options, one right answer), three questions per period,

  (challenge under 7 days or 2 periods, each word language under 2 periods).

  paid in the door; never points or gems; a finished play (solved or not) keeps the daily streak; amounts are data.

  dropped, ة ى ء ؤ ئ kept); guesses only from the list or the day's word, a refused one uses no try; one game per period
  in one language; the word shown only at the end.
