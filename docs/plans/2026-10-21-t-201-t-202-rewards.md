# T-201 + T-202 — Rewards builder, shop, reserve and cancel

Delivers FR-501 to FR-505, FR-514 to FR-516, FR-1618 (T-201) and FR-506, FR-508, FR-513, FR-1603, FR-1609 (T-202), in
part FR-1717. ADR-043 (spending lowers the balance only), ADR-048 (the shop window after the event), ADR-049 (reserve in
the app, pick up at the desk). **Max bar:** every reserve, cancel and return writes the points ledger (core). One CTO
cycle for both tasks: T-201's "done when" (a reward with stock 1 reserved by one player only) is already T-202's
reserve.

## Scope

- **In:** the reward table and its builder on the dashboard (create, edit, remove, stock, sections, tags, window,
  per-player limit, kinds item and experience); the event's shop settings (pickup time, shop window after the end,
  official shop rules); the player's shop (balance, sections, tags, real stock, sold out, the rules before a first
  redemption); reserve (price and one unit taken at once, in the ledger); cancel by the player; automatic return when
  the pickup time passes, on the lifecycle tick.
- **Later, with a note on each task:** the digital-code kind and its codes (T-203: the kind joins the CHECK there; no
  phantom kind now); handover and operator cancel at the desk, desk location and hours, the open-flag hold (T-206;
  FR-1717's desk-hours half); reward pictures (no object storage code exists yet: the upload path lands with T-602's
  branding uploads; until then a reward shows its kind's brand icon); quest and category item rewards out of stock
  (FR-1617, with T-304's completion bonus); prizes on the event web page (FR-115, a T-112 note); the shop on/off and
  one-step desk redemption (FR-1834, T-610's customization set); the balance closing after the window (ADR-048, with
  T-214's final results).

## Decisions (tactical; for the CTO to challenge)

1. **`reward`:** `id, organizer_id, event_id, kind (item|experience), name i18n, description i18n, price (1 to
   1 000 000), stock_total (0 to 100 000), units_out (0 ≤ units_out ≤ stock_total), per_player_limit (null or 1 to
   100), opens_at, closes_at (null = the shop's own window), section (featured|merch|food|experiences or null), tag_new,
   tag_limited, position, status (active|removed), created_at`. Remaining stock is `stock_total − units_out`, so it
   never drifts: `units_out` moves only inside the definer functions, and the CHECK refuses an organizer edit that would
   put the total below the units out (409 `stock_below_reserved`). At most 100 rewards per event. Removing a reward
   hides it from the shop; its reservations stay collectible until their deadline.
2. **`redemption`:** `id, organizer_id, event_id, enrollment_id, reward_id, price (what was paid), status
   reserved|handed_over|cancelled|expired, idempotency_key, reserved_until, ended_at, ended_by (player|timeout),
   created_at`; unique `(enrollment_id, idempotency_key)`; index `(reserved_until) WHERE status = 'reserved'` for the
   tick and `(reward_id, status)`. Rows are never deleted. `handed_over` is in the CHECK for T-206 (the status the
   per-player limit counts) but nothing writes it yet.
3. **The ledger:** two new kinds, each moving the balance only (ADR-043): `redeem` (balance −price, score 0) and
   `redeem_return` (balance +price, score 0), both with a `redemption_id` (new column, FK) and CHECKs on the signs;
   unique partial indexes on `redemption_id` per kind, so one reservation is paid and returned at most once. `actor`
   widens to `system` for the timeout. The ranking ignores both (its version reads `score_delta <> 0`).
4. **Event shop settings** (columns on `event`): `pickup_minutes` (15 to 10 080, default 120), `shop_days_after` (0 to
   30, default 3, ADR-048), `shop_rules` i18n (up to 2000 characters, optional). `PUT …/events/{eventId}/shop` (owner
   or editor within their events; any status but archived; audited `event.shop.update`). The event view carries
   `shop`.
5. **When the shop is open:** the event is live, or ended less than `shop_days_after` days ago. A reward redeems only
   inside its own window too (`opens_at`/`closes_at`, FR-503). A reservation made before the shop closes keeps its own
   `reserved_until` (FR-1609).
6. **`reserve_reward(target, request_key)`** (definer, the player from the request context): lock order enrollment
   (FOR NO KEY UPDATE) → reward (FOR UPDATE). A replay of the key returns the first redemption; the key used for
   another reward is QK422. Refusals: not found / not enrolled RN404; enrollment not active RA403; shop closed RS403
   (DETAIL `not_open|closed`); reward window RW403 (DETAIL `not_started|ended`, the times in the answer); sold out
   RO409; per-player limit RL409; balance RB422. Writes the redemption, `units_out + 1` and the `redeem` entry; returns
   the redemption, the balance and the stock left.
7. **`cancel_redemption(target)`** (player): lock order enrollment → reward → redemption. Only `reserved` cancels:
   `cancelled`, `units_out − 1`, a `redeem_return` entry. Already cancelled: answers the redemption as it is
   (idempotent, no second entry); handed over or expired: RE409 (DETAIL the status). Another player's: RN404.
8. **`expire_reservations(size)`** (definer, no context, EXECUTE for `questify_app` like `advance_event_lifecycle`):
   picks up to `size` ids with `reserved_until <= now()` without locking, then for each takes the same lock order and
   re-checks the status before writing `expired`, `units_out − 1` and a `redeem_return` (actor `system`). Runs on the
   lifecycle tick after `advance_event_lifecycle()`, 200 per run; logs the count only.
9. **Reads:** organizers read `reward` and `redemption` in their scope (RLS, like quests); players never read the tables
   directly: `event_shop(target)` (definer, a member only) returns named columns of the active rewards with stock left,
   and the shop state; `my_redemptions(target)` their own. Popular (FR-1618): the 3 rewards with the most reserved or
   handed-over units, at least 3 each, computed in the read.
10. **The API:** organizer `GET/POST …/events/{eventId}/rewards` (POST with Idempotency-Key), `PATCH/DELETE
    …/rewards/{rewardId}`, `PUT …/events/{eventId}/shop`; player `GET /v1/events/{id}/shop` → `{shop: {open, opensFor,
    closesAt, pickupMinutes, rules, firstRedemption}, balance, rewards: [...], redemptions: [...]}`, `POST
    /v1/rewards/{id}/reserve` (Idempotency-Key, a new key per tap), `POST /v1/redemptions/{id}/cancel`. Errors carry
    Arabic and English messages like the rest.
11. **FR-515, the rules before a first redemption:** `firstRedemption` is true while the player has no redemption in
    the event (any status). The app shows the rules sheet (the organizer's `shop_rules`, else Questify's standard
    text with the event's pickup time and cancel rule) before the first Redeem; the server does not gate on it (it is
    information, not consent).
12. **The app's Rewards tab:** balance, section chips, reward cards (price with the event's currency icon, stock left or
    Sold out, tags), a reward sheet with Redeem → confirm (rules first when first) → reserved ("pick up at the prize
    desk within {time}"), My reservations with time left and Cancel (confirm). Copy through `ux-araby`.
13. **The dashboard:** a Rewards page beside Quests: the list (stock left, units out), a form (the fields above), remove
    with confirmation, and the shop settings card. A browser test for create, edit stock, remove.
