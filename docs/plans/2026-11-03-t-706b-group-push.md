# T-706b - Push to a group of players (plan note, tier B)

Delivers FR-1777 and the push half of the T-706 notes. Migration **0138**. ADR-530 to ADR-535, Q-490 to Q-493 (Proposed).
The rest of the old T-706b is split off as **T-706c** (ADR-530).

## The split

T-706b as written holds four things: push to a group, promotional campaigns, the promotional email sender, and the senders
reading their template slot. The last three share one purpose (promotional SMS and email, with a new outside call and the
sign-in path) and nothing in them is needed to send a push.

- **T-706b (this)**: an admin sends a push to everyone in an event, or to opted-in players by city or interest, through the
  existing push pipeline. Reads the `promo_push` slot as the composer's default text. Decides Q-421 (the cap) and builds it
  for push.
- **T-706c**: promotional SMS and email campaigns (recipients through `marketing_audience`, SMS scheduled with `promoSendTime`,
  quiet hours SMS only), the promotional email sender (decided in ADR-535), the promotional SMS, email and login-code senders
  reading their slot. The login-code slot is on the sign-in path: tier A for that part (Q-420).

## Design

1. **Two kinds** in `notice_kind`, both free text (`FREE_TEXT_KINDS`, the announcements' renderer and name rule):
   `group_push_event` (event class, topic `announcements`, so a muted topic keeps the center row and loses the push) and
   `group_push_promo` (promo class, no topic, per player). The app's route table sends both to the center.
2. **`group_push`**: one row per send (audience, event or city and interests, the text in both languages, the admin, state
   `sending` or `done`, counts). Closed to every role; read and written by admin-checked doors and the fan door.
3. **Event audience** is one `notice_create('group_push_event', ...)`: the push worker's existing event fan-out reaches the
   event's enrolled players in batches, with its status, topic and suspension checks. `notice_status_ok` gains one rule for
   this kind: real members of a scheduled, live or ended event (never rehearsal accounts, never a draft or archived event).
4. **Opted-in audience** is per player: `group_push_fan()` (system seat, a door of the push worker's loop, run before its
   fan-out so a failure is logged with its SQLSTATE and never stops the fan-out) takes the
   oldest `sending` row `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`, reads 500 players after its cursor through `marketing_audience('push', city,
   interests, ...)`, skips a player already told this week (the cap), writes one `group_push_promo` notice each
   (`gpush:{id}:{player}`, so a repeat writes nothing) and advances the cursor. Consent is read again by the push store at the
   fan-out and at the claim (`push_consented`), so an opt-out between the audience page and the phone still wins.
5. **Cap (Q-421)**: `promo_push_free(player)` is true when the player has no invitation row on the push channel and no group
   promo notice in the last 7 days. The group fan asks it, and `invitation_candidates` asks the mirror of it, so the two
   features together send a player at most one promotional push a week.
6. **Doors**, all `console_admin()` first (QE403): `admin_group_push_preview` (resolves the event by join code or counts the
   audience; read only), `admin_group_push_send` (checks the text and the audience again, writes the row and, for an event,
   the notice; audited `group_push.send` with the audience and counts, never the text's recipients), `admin_group_push_list`
   (the last 50). Limits per admin: 10 sends an hour (API bucket) and, in the door, 20 an hour over all admins.
7. **Text**: `checkGroupPushText` in the shared package (both languages in their own script, title at most 60 and text at most
   140 characters, no placeholder, the announcements' cleaning and name rule). The door checks length, control characters
   and braces again.
8. **Console**: Messages page, third tab **Send a push**: audience (an event by join code, or players by city and interests),
   text prefilled from the saved promotional push, a preview with the reach and the event's name, a confirm dialog, and the
   list of earlier sends with their counts.
9. **Deny by default**: the table closed (RLS forced, no grant); three admin doors granted to the API role; the fan door granted
   (its seat check is the system context); helpers closed. Outsider, cross-tenant and route-table entries added. No player
   write route is added, so the maintenance-doors walk needs no entry (verified by running it).

## Tests

`packages/shared` group-push rules; `test/db/group-push` (door refusals for every seat, text and audience refusals, the event
reach by status and membership, the promo reach by consent, city, interest and cap, a repeat of the fan, an opt-out between
the audience read and the fan-out, the invitation side of the cap, erasure); `test/console/group-push` (HTTP: 404 to team
members, 401 signed out, refusals with fields named, send, list, rate limit); push worker unit (the fan door is called, a
failure is logged and the loop goes on); security suites; `e2e/console-messaging.spec.ts` extended.

## Not built here

Cancel of a running send; a scheduled send time (push has no quiet hours, Q-287); a per-city count in the preview beyond the
total; promotional SMS and email (T-706c).
