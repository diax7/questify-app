# T-206 — Prize desk

Delivers FR-507, FR-509, FR-925, FR-926, FR-1716, and the parts left open by T-201/T-202: the operator's cancel
(FR-513, FR-1603) and the desk's last hours (FR-1717). **Max bar:** prize handover (QUALITY_BASELINE). Migration
**0029**. Depends on T-202 (reservations) and T-205 (My QR, operators).

## Decisions from Dhia (2026-10-23) → ADR-150, Accepted

1. **The player confirms in their own app (ADR-137), and the owner picks how per event** (`handover_confirm`):
   - `collect_first` (default): in the queue the player taps **Collect** on a reservation; it is ready for 5 minutes
     and My QR shows "Collecting: Hoodie". The desk scans, sees the item **Ready**, taps **Handed over**: final.
   - `desk_offer`: the desk scans and taps **Hand over**; the player's My QR screen shows **Accept / Not now** for 2
     minutes; the handover is final when the player accepts.
2. **No undo** after a handover (drops the 10-second Undo of OPERATOR_MODE 3.3). A handover is final, once.
3. **Desk hours are optional.** None set: the desk is always open. Set (several windows, up to 14): items **and
   experiences** are handed over or confirmed only inside a window, and are no longer reservable after the last window
   ends. Digital codes never go through the desk and keep FR-1717's rule (redeemable until the shop closes).
   Amends FR-1716 (hours optional) and FR-1717 (experiences follow the desk too).

## Scope

- **In:** the event's desk (location AR/EN, optional pin, optional hours, confirmation mode) on the dashboard's
  Rewards page; the desk tab in operator mode (scan My QR, the player's waiting items, Handed over / Hand over /
  Confirm, Cancel with a confirm screen, nothing waiting, desk closed, not ready, waiting for accept, refusals); the
  player's Collect, the accept sheet on My QR, desk place and hours on the reserve sheet and on each reservation,
  `handed_over` in the history; the operator's cancel; desk hours closing desk rewards.
- **Out:** the flag hold and the owner's pause (FR-1815, FR-518, FR-1604) → T-209, which hooks a check into the one
  handover body below. Rank prizes and quest item rewards at the desk (FR-521, FR-522) → T-214 / later. Find by name or
  phone when a code cannot be read (OPERATOR_MODE note, no FR in T-206) → noted on T-209 (desk) — the player can reset
  My QR or open it again meanwhile. Push for an offer: none; the player is standing at the desk with My QR open.

## Data (migration 0029)

1. **`event`** gains, in the API role's UPDATE grant (written by the shop settings route, like `pickup_minutes`):
   - `desk_location_ar`, `desk_location_en` (1–200), `desk_lat`, `desk_lng` (both or neither, valid ranges);
   - `desk_hours tstzmultirange` — NULL = always open. A CHECK through an IMMUTABLE helper: not empty, every window
     bounded, at most 14 windows (a multirange merges overlaps itself; the API refuses overlaps before that);
   - `handover_confirm text NOT NULL DEFAULT 'collect_first' CHECK IN ('collect_first', 'desk_offer')`.
   - **FR-1716 guard** (triggers, both sides): inserting an `item` reward reads its event `FOR SHARE` (no write skew
     with a clear) and needs a desk location (`DL409`; `kind` is not updatable, so INSERT is enough); on `event`, only
     the change of the location from set to NULL is refused while an active item reward exists (`DL409`), so a
     legacy event without a desk still saves its other settings. The API refuses that transition up front too.
     Experiences need no location (an experiences-only event has an always-open desk with no place; ADR-151).
2. **`desk_state(hours tstzmultirange)`** STABLE → `open` (NULL, or a window holds now()), `closed` (a later window
   exists), `finished` (every window is past). The one place this rule lives, like `shop_state`.
3. **`redemption`**: status adds `handed_over`; `ended_by` adds `handover` and `operator` (an operator's cancel);
   new `operator_id uuid` (who handed over or cancelled; set exactly when `ended_by IN ('handover','operator')`);
   `collect_until timestamptz` (the player's Collect) and `offer_by uuid`, `offer_until timestamptz` (the desk's offer;
   both or neither). The three are set only on a `reserved` row (CHECK). A partial index for the player's open items.
   Shape CHECKs: `(status = 'handed_over') = (ended_by IS NOT DISTINCT FROM 'handover')`; `ended_by = 'operator'` →
   `status = 'cancelled'`; `(ended_by IN ('handover','operator')) = (operator_id IS NOT NULL)`.
   (`redemption_delivered_shape` already keeps `reserved_until` on a handed-over row.)
4. **Ledger** (the points record): `ledger_entry_kind_check` rewritten from 0028's list plus `handover` (`welcome`
   kept). `ledger_entry_handover_shape`: kind `handover` → `redemption_id` NOT NULL, both deltas 0, `completion_id` and
   `quest_id` NULL, `actor = 'operator'` (with the handing operator's id); one per redemption (unique partial index).
   `redeem_return` may now have `actor = 'operator'` (the operator's cancel). `ledger_entry_operator_quests_only`
   becomes `actor <> 'operator' OR kind IN ('quest', 'handover', 'redeem_return')`; `redemption_only_spend` widens to
   `handover`. `member_activity` already names any redemption-linked entry (0 points; the app shows "Picked up").
5. **Reserving, again** (`reserve_reward` recreated from 0025 with three changes):
   - the per-player limit counts `handed_over` (`status NOT IN ('cancelled','expired')`);
   - an `item` or `experience` with `desk_state = 'finished'` → `RS403` detail `desk_finished`;
   - with hours set and the desk closed now, the pickup clock starts at the next opening:
     `reserved_until = greatest(now(), next window start) + pickup_minutes` (else a reservation made overnight expires
     before the desk opens). `shop_rewards.held_by_me` counts `handed_over` too.
6. **One ending body**: `end_reservation(target, outcome, by_whom, who, operator uuid)` replaces the 4-argument one
   (dropped; `cancel_redemption` and `expire_one_reservation` recreated to call it). Same lock order (enrollment,
   reward, redemption); still total: any non-`reserved` row → false, never a raise on state. Clears collect/offer.
   For `outcome = 'expired'` it re-checks under the lock that `reserved_until <= now()` and that no collect/offer is
   live, else false (a Collect that commits between expiry's unlocked pick and its lock wins). Not callable by any role.
7. **`expire_one_reservation`** skips a row that is ready or offered right now (`collect_until`/`offer_until` > now()):
   the player is at the desk. Still the oldest due row otherwise; the skipped row is picked up once its 5 or 2 minutes
   pass, so nothing stalls.
   **Deadlines (FR-1609):** `start_collect` and a desk offer or handover refuse a row whose `reserved_until` has
   passed (`RE409`, detail `expired`) even before the tick ends it. A Collect or offer already live may finish past
   the deadline (ADR-151). Desk hours bound FR-1609: a reservation made 5 minutes before the last window ends is
   collectible for those 5 minutes (ADR-151; boundary tested).
8. **One handover body, every gate in it**: `finish_handover(target, operator uuid, expected_offer boolean)
   RETURNS text` (no grant), shared by both paths, so T-209 adds its hold here and nowhere else. In order:
   the operator's `staff_assignment` `FOR SHARE` with `hand_over` (`OP403`/`OA403`: a removed operator's live offer
   cannot be accepted, FR-905); then enrollment `FOR NO KEY UPDATE`, reward `FOR UPDATE`, redemption `FOR UPDATE`;
   then under the locks: `reserved` (else the status; same operator on a handed-over row → `replayed`), not past its
   deadline unless a collect/offer is live, enrollment `active` (`RA403`), not the operator's own (`OY409`),
   `desk_state = 'open'` (`RS403 desk_closed`), the event's mode, and on the accept path `offer_by = operator` with
   `offer_until > now()` (`RO410`); on the desk path in `collect_first`, `collect_until > now()` (`RC409`). Then sets
   `handed_over`, `ended_at`, `ended_by = 'handover'`, `operator_id`, clears collect/offer, writes the `handover`
   ledger entry; units stay out (FR-1603 never returns a handed-over unit).
   **Lock order everywhere:** assignment `FOR SHARE` → enrollment → reward → redemption (as `operator_complete_quest`
   and `end_reservation`). `start_collect`, `decline_handover` and the offer write (`desk_hand_over` in `desk_offer`
   mode, after `desk_operator`) take the redemption row lock only and read the enrollment unlocked, so none of them can
   hold the redemption while waiting on the enrollment.
9. **Definers** (`SECURITY DEFINER`, pinned search_path, `REVOKE … FROM PUBLIC`, `GRANT … TO questify_app`; the caller
   always from `app_player_id()`):
   - `desk_operator(target_event)` (internal, no grant): the caller's assignment `FOR SHARE` (a removal waits, then
     refuses, FR-905): `OP403` not an operator, `OA403` `hand_over` off.
   - `desk_lookup(target_event, scanned, scanned_version)` → the player's username, avatar, enrollment status, the
     event's desk state and mode, and their `reserved` desk items (id, kind, name AR/EN, price, created_at,
     reserved_until, `ready` = collect_until > now(), `offered` = offer_until > now()). Refusals: `OY409` own code,
     `OM422` old My QR, `QC404` not in the event. Digital rewards never appear. Reads only; never touches quests (FR-926).
   - `desk_hand_over(target)` → `handed_over` or `offered` (+ offer_until), `replayed`:
     the redemption's event → `desk_operator`; `RE409` not reserved (DETAIL status; a repeat by the same operator on a
     handed-over row answers it again, `replayed`); `RA403` enrollment not active; `OY409` the operator's own
     reservation; `RS403 desk_closed`. Mode `collect_first`: `RC409` not ready (no live Collect) else `finish_handover`.
     Mode `desk_offer`: sets `offer_by`, `offer_until = now() + 2 min` (a new offer replaces an older one). The
     offer write's checks are unlocked on purpose: `finish_handover` re-checks every one under the locks on accept.
   - `desk_redemption(target)` → status, offered, offer_until — the desk's 2-second poll while it waits (operator of
     that event only). `offered` is true only when `offer_by` is the caller: another desk's newer offer reads as
     "Not accepted" here.
   - `operator_cancel_redemption(target)` — `desk_operator`; `end_reservation(…, 'cancelled', 'operator', 'operator',
     me)`; same answers as the player's cancel. Allowed while the desk is closed.
   - Player: `start_collect(target)` (`RN404` not mine; `RE409` not reserved or past its deadline; `RM409` the event
     is in `desk_offer` mode; `RA403` enrollment not active; `RS403 desk_closed` while the desk is closed or finished)
     → collect_until = now() + 5 min. `accept_handover(target)` (`RN404`; mode must be `desk_offer`, `RM409`; reads
     `offer_by` unlocked, then `finish_handover(target, offer_by, true)`, which re-checks the offer under the lock;
     a repeat answers `replayed`). `decline_handover(target)` clears the offer.
   - A switch of `handover_confirm` while a Collect or offer is live: the other path refuses it with `RM409`; nothing
     is cleared (one test). `my_desk_items()` → the caller's reserved rows, across
     events, that are ready or offered now (event name, reward name, kind, state, until) for My QR.
   - `event_shop` and `my_redemptions` gain the desk's location, pin, hours, state and mode (FR-1716: shown on every
     item reservation), and `my_redemptions` the collect/offer times and `handed_over`.

## API

- Shop settings (`PUT /v1/organizers/{o}/events/{e}/shop`, member): `desk: {locationAr?, locationEn?, pin?: {lat, lng}
  | null, hours: [{opensAt, closesAt}] | null} | null`, `handoverConfirm`. Zod: ≤ 14 windows, each closes > opens,
  sorted and not overlapping, at least one location name; `DL409` → 409 `desk_needed` (item rewards exist).
  Creating an item reward without a desk → 409 `desk_needed` (dashboard shows "Set the prize desk first").
- New module `apps/api/src/desk/` (`desk.ts`, `routes.ts`) — operator routes use the `player` access kind like the
  station:
  - `GET /v1/operator/events/{eventId}/desk` → `{desk: {location, pin, hours, state}, confirm}`.
  - `POST /v1/operator/desk/lookup {eventId, code}` → `verifyMyCode` in the API (bad or tampered → `invalid_code`),
    then `desk_lookup` → `{result: "found", player, enrollment, items[]}` or `{result: "refused", reason}` (reasons:
    `invalid_code`, `old_code`, `not_in_event`, `self_scan`, `ability_off`); `not_operator` → 403.
  - `POST /v1/operator/redemptions/{id}/hand-over` (**Idempotency-Key**) → `{status: handed_over | offered,
    offerUntil?, replayed}`; 403 `not_operator` / `ability_off` / `desk_closed` / `enrollment_inactive`; 409
    `not_ready` / `reservation_ended {status}` / `self_handover`.
  - `GET /v1/operator/redemptions/{id}` → `{status, offered, offerUntil}`.
  - `POST /v1/operator/redemptions/{id}/cancel` (**Idempotency-Key**) → as the player's cancel.
  - Player: `POST /v1/redemptions/{id}/collect` → `{collectUntil}`; `POST …/accept` (**Idempotency-Key**) →
    `{status: handed_over, replayed}`; `POST …/decline` → 204; `GET /v1/me/desk-items` → `{items}`.
  - The shop and reservation answers gain `desk` and the new statuses. No route on the desk returns points or touches
    the ranking (FR-926).

- **Desk tab** (replaces the ComingSoon placeholder): header with the desk's state ("Open until 18:00", "Closed: opens
  Thu 10:00", "Open the whole time"); the camera (the station's scanner reused) → lookup → the player card with items:
  - item → **Handed over** (collect_first, ready) / **Hand over** (desk_offer); experience → **Confirm**; each with
    **Cancel** → confirm screen "Cancel this reservation? {player} gets the {currency} back." [Cancel reservation]
    [Keep];

- **Player**: the reserve sheet and each reservation show the desk place and hours; a reservation gets **Collect**

## Tests (test-first)

- **DB** (`apps/api/test/desk/desk.int.test.ts`): both modes end to end; the limit counting `handed_over`; desk
  finished refusing items and experiences but not digital; overnight reservation clock; `desk_state` boundaries;
  FR-1716 guard both ways; every refusal code; FR-926 (a desk lookup and handover leave score, balance and
  completions untouched); handover writes exactly one `handover` entry, a cancel exactly one `redeem_return` with the
  operator; a removed operator refused at once; expiry skips a ready row and takes it after.
  **Races** (two connections): handover vs the player's cancel, handover vs expiry, accept vs the operator's cancel,
  two desks handing over the same row, operator removed vs accept, Collect vs expiry — exactly one wins, units and
  balance consistent. Also: Collect on an overdue row refused; a banned enrollment or a closed window refuses an
  accept; `end_reservation` with `who = 'operator'` and no operator fails on `ledger_entry_operator_named` (once).
- **Outsider** (`test/security/outsider.int.test.ts`): every new function refused to a role without `questify_app`
  and to PUBLIC, enumerated by name, internal ones included (`desk_state`, the hours helper, both guard trigger
  functions, `finish_handover`, `desk_operator`, the 5-argument `end_reservation`; the 4-argument one gone from the
  lists); the new routes without a session → 401; a player who is not an operator → 403.
- **Cross-tenant**: an operator of event A cannot look up, hand over, poll or cancel in event B.
- **API** route tests for the bodies, the code verification and the error mapping; web `lib/rewards` tests; app
  `desk.ts` tests.
- **Mutation** (core): `apps/api/src/desk` and the shop settings' desk parse.
