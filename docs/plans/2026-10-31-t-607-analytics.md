# T-607 - Analytics (plan note)

Tier B: three read-only reads of existing rows; no point is written, nothing about sign-in, the points record or tenant isolation changes
(each read sits behind `award_event_of`, as the control room's). Delivers FR-1201 to FR-1205 and FR-1625. Migration **0102** (staging runs
through 0101). Proposed ADR-392, Q-359.

## What is built

- Three definer doors (`analytics_participation`, `analytics_quests`, `analytics_economy`), no table, no index; one route
  `GET …/events/{id}/analytics/{panel}` that checks each answer against a strict schema (counts only) before it leaves.
- The page `/analytics`: tiles, then equally tall cards; lists of five with "View more"; plain SVG and CSS (no new dependency).
- Definitions: joined = ever a member (not pending or declined); active = at least one completion; activation = active over joined;
  returning = a completion on two or more event days (`event_streak_day`); a completion whose award the team removed does not count as
  active or toward a quest (unless the player has another that stands); earned = score cache + the score each standing redemption took
  (its `redeem` entry; a purchase made after the end took none); spent = standing reward redemptions; unclaimed = reservations not picked up.

## Performance (the stated limit: each panel under about 1 s at 50,000 players, no full ledger scan per request)

The points record is never scanned. Participation reads the event's enrollments and probes `quest_completion` by `enrollment_id` once per
player (index only), the quests read the partial index of first completions (0096), the economy reads the score cache and the rewards'
redemptions. Two small reads of `ledger_entry` remain, none ordered by players: the event's removals (through `ledger_note`, skipping a
bulk award's notes) take the removed completions off active and off a quest's players, and each standing redemption's own `redeem` entry
(`ledger_entry_redeem_key`) is added back to earned. Measured (`apps/api/test/perf/analytics-timing.int.test.ts`, 50,000 players, 35,000
active, 525,000 completions, 1,000 removals, 50,000 bulk-award notes, 3,000 redemptions): participation 370 to 635 ms, quests 143 to 192 ms,
economy 27 to 51 ms. No cache is added.
