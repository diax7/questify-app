# T-113 — Event Home and the event bottom bar

Delivers FR-801, FR-802, FR-804, FR-805 (in part), FR-620, FR-623, FR-624, FR-1610, FR-1748; FR-621 and FR-622 (the
scanner and My QR behind the center button) are T-115's, which this task leaves a place for. Standard bar for the
screens; the members-only quest read is tenant isolation (Max).

## Scope note

- **Server:** players have no read of quests yet (0010: "players read quests through their own API"); Home's Up next and
  T-114's Quests tab both need one. Events have no pinned quest (FR-1748), no estimated time per quest, no banner, about,
  address, FAQ or site fields.
- **App:** the five tabs today are Home, Quests, Ranking, Shop, Profile with placeholders; T-111 added the Questify
  Home (code entry, public list, my events).
- **Not here:** the scanner and My QR (T-115), the 11-row ranking screen's final form (T-116: this task opens a plain
  version of it), the banner and branding (T-602), the event's about, address, FAQ and rules fields (T-214 writes the
  rules; the rest has no task yet: Q-099), "nearest" for Up next (needs location, T-3xx), the goal (FR-801's "the
  player's goal" needs rewards, T-201).

## Decisions (tactical; for the CTO to challenge)

1. **The members' quest read:** `member_event_quests(event uuid)` (definer, pinned, closed to PUBLIC) for an
   `active` or `pending` member of the event (`app_player_id()`): its categories and visible quests by named columns
   (never `type_config`), each with how many times this player completed it and its repeat cap. `GET
   /v1/events/{id}/quests` answers it (403 `not_enrolled` otherwise); T-114 renders it.
2. **Home in one call:** `GET /v1/events/{id}/home` (player, member) → `{event: {id, name, venue, startsAt, endsAt,
   timezone, statusNow}, standing: {status, score, balance}, upNext: {questId, title, points, type} | null, rankingOn}`.
   Up next (FR-804, FR-1748): the pinned quest when it is open and not done to its cap by this player; else the open,
   not-finished quest with the most points (ties by position). "Open" means visible, inside its window, the event live.
3. **The pin (FR-1748):** `event.focus_quest_id` (nullable; the same event, enforced by a composite FK; cleared when
   the quest is deleted). `PUT …/events/{eventId}/focus {questId | null}` (owner or editor within scope) and a "Pin as
   Up next" switch per quest in the dashboard's builder. Audited.
4. **The bar (FR-620):** the in-event tabs become Home, Quests, a large round center button (Scan: T-115's scanner;
   until then it opens a "coming soon" sheet), Rewards (the Shop placeholder), Profile, mirrored in Arabic by the
   layout. Ranking leaves the bar: the Home card opens it (FR-802).
5. **Inside an event or on Questify home (FR-624, FR-1610, FR-623):** the app keeps, per player on the phone, the
   current event and when each event was last opened. At launch: the most recently used of the player's events that
   is live now (from `/v1/me/events`); none → Questify home (T-111's Home). The event pill at the top of the event Home
   opens a sheet with "Questify home" and the player's other events; one tap on an entry goes there (two taps to leave).
6. **Home (FR-801):** the event's name with a countdown (to the start while scheduled, to the end while live, "ended"
   after), the player's points and balance, the ranking card (`GET …/ranking`: my rank and the top 3; hidden on 404
   `ranking_off`; tapping opens the ranking screen), and Up next with Go (opens the quest in the Quests tab; T-114
   shows its sheet). A pending member sees "waiting for approval" instead of progress.
7. **The ranking screen (FR-802):** a plain list of the top 10 and the player's own row from the same API; T-116 makes
   it fit 11 rows on every size and adds the profile details.
8. **The info page (FR-805, in part):** name, venue (opening the maps app with the venue text), dates in the event's
   zone, and the official rules link when T-214 adds it; the missing sections are Q-099.

## Data

Migration 0021: `event.focus_quest_id` with the composite FK and `ON DELETE SET NULL` (through a trigger, as the FK is
on `(focus_quest_id, id)` → `quest (id, event_id)`), the grant for the column, `member_event_quests`, `event_home`
(the definer that computes standing and Up next for the calling member).

## Tests

- Database: the members' read (an active and a pending member see it; a left, removed or banned one, another
  organizer's player and an outsider do not; no `type_config`; the player's own completions only); Up next (pinned,
  pinned but done or closed, best points, ties, none open, a scheduled event); the pin's same-event rule and its
  clearing when the quest goes.
- HTTP: both player routes and the pin route with their errors; the route inventory; the cross-tenant classification.
- App (Vitest): the launch choice (most recent live event; none live; an event no longer in the list), the countdown
  text, the Up next and ranking-card mapping.
- Mutation on the new API modules.

## Revision 1 (after CTO plan review 1, 79/100) — overrides the sections above where they differ

- **B1, active members only:** `member_event_quests` answers only for an `active` enrollment of an `active` player;
  a pending member (only private events make them) is refused like a stranger (FR-1819: they see the name and
  "waiting for approval"). `GET …/quests` answers 403 `not_enrolled` to them; `/home` returns the event and
  `standing.status: pending` with no score and `upNext: null`. Tested.
- **M1, the bar stays left to right:** the ADR-110 note (user, 2026-10-20) keeps the tab order in both languages; the
  bar stays `direction: ltr`. FR-620's and FR-830's "mirrored in Arabic" go to OPEN_QUESTIONS (Q-100) instead of being
  overridden either way.
- **M2, the route tree:** `app/index.tsx` decides at launch and redirects; `app/home.tsx` is Questify home (T-111's
  code entry, public list, my events; its own bar is T-408); `app/event/[eventId]/(tabs)/{index,quests,scan,rewards,
  profile}.tsx` is the event; `app/event/[eventId]/ranking.tsx` and `info.tsx` are stack screens over it. The event id
  lives in the route; every tab reads it from params. Signed out, launch goes to Questify home. After a join, the card
  lands on `/event/{id}`. The center tab is a `tabPress` listener with `preventDefault` that opens the sheet. The old
  `(tabs)` folder goes (replace, not keep). The in-event Profile is the player's own profile (the same screen as
  today's), with the language and sign-out.
- **M3, Home's states:** loading; a network error with retry; 403 `not_enrolled` (removed, banned or left meanwhile):
  the event is forgotten on the phone and the app goes to Questify home; 404: the same; ended: the countdown says so
  and Up next is hidden; a refetch on focus and when the app comes to the foreground (points after a scan), and when
  the countdown crosses zero (the server says when the status changes, not the phone's clock). Each mapping has a
  Vitest case.
- **m1, no second definer:** Home is composed in TypeScript: the event and standing from a scoped enrollment read
  (as `/v1/me/events`), the quests from `member_event_quests`, and Up next from a pure `pickUpNext(quests, focusQuestId,
  statusNow, now)`, one definition of "open" that T-114 reuses for open and locked. Mutation-tested.
- **m2:** `FOREIGN KEY (focus_quest_id, id) REFERENCES quest (id, event_id) ON DELETE SET NULL (focus_quest_id)`
  (PostgreSQL 17), no trigger; tests: deleting a pinned quest clears the pin; deleting a draft event with a pin works.
- **m3:** FR-1748's "points per estimated minute" becomes "most points" until quests have an estimated time: Q-101.
- **m4, the pin route:** `PUT …/events/{eventId}/focus` with `z.strictObject({questId: z.uuid().nullable()})`; another
  event's or an unknown quest (23503) is 404; an ended or archived event is 409 `event_closed`; the builder's event
  read includes `focusQuestId`; audited as `event.focus_set` with before and after; a hidden quest may be pinned and
  its card says it will not show in Up next while hidden.
- **m5:** Home's answer carries `rankingOn`; the app skips the ranking call when it is off and still hides the card on
  a 404 `ranking_off`; a pending member never calls it.
- **m6:** the event pill sits in every event tab's header.
- **m7, the launch rule:** active enrollments whose event is live now; the most recently opened on this phone; with no
  record, the `/me/events` order (newest join first); a failed `/me/events` goes to Questify home with a visible retry.
  The recency map is per player and cleared at sign-out. The T-111 `MyEvents` failure that showed "no events" is fixed.
- **m8, progress:** Home shows the points and the balance and `questsDone` of `questsTotal` (visible quests).
- **m9:** the countdown is written with ux-araby (Arabic dual and plural forms), Western digits, the event's zone
  through the shared time helper; Hermes's `Intl` time-zone support is checked.
- **Nits:** one map `eventId → lastOpenedAt` (the current event is its latest); the "coming soon" sheet never reaches
  a store build (T-115 lands before T-118); the builder's pin is a toggle with `aria-pressed` and a "Pinned" badge,
  moving without confirmation; maps open with `https://maps.apple.com/?q=` on iOS and `geo:0,0?q=` on Android.
