# T-309b — Kiosk: the live code, the kiosk page and the kiosk door

F-107, FR-315: a tablet paired as a **kiosk** for one quest scans each player's **live code** and pays them with no
staff present. The design was fixed and CTO-reviewed in the T-309 plan (`docs/plans/2026-10-24-t-309-stations-kiosks-pairing.md`,
section "Kiosk", ADR-242, Q-201); this plan builds it on what T-309 actually shipped and records where building it
showed a gap ("Changes from the fixed design"). **Core bar**: a new signed proof (0x08), a door that pays a player with
no person watching, and a public screen: one Opus plan review, one Opus code review, mutation on GitHub.
Migration **0058** (reserved; CranL runs through 0056 and 0057 is undeployed but finished and reviewed, so its
functions are changed here by restating them, never by editing 0057). Depends on T-309 (pairing, the device credential,
the screen window), T-205 (the station scan this mirrors), T-211 (the go-live wipe), T-209 (burst flags), all done.

Dhia is away: open choices are my recommendation, written as **Proposed** ADR-244 and ADR-245 and Q-207 and Q-208.
FR-315's rewording ("live code" for "live My QR") stays **pending Q-201**.

## What T-309 built that this plan builds on

- **Migration 0057**: `paired_device` (kind `kiosk` allowed, `quest_id` required), `device_alive` (active, event not
  ended more than 24 h ago, seen within 30 days), `current_device()` (the live, activated device of `app_device_id()`,
  else DV401), `device_paused(d)` (an operator-paired kiosk pauses when its operator loses `scan_quests` or the quest
  leaves their limit; CTO 3 of T-309), `screen_window()` / `screen_window_of()` / `screen_window_ok(version, at)` (the
  30-second window on the database clock, the previous one for 15 s), `device_view()` (the paired page's view and its
  `shut` reason), `record_completion` and `complete_quest` restated with `screen_qr` and the one-pay-per-window rule
  (`quest_completion_screen_idx`), the removal triggers, `operator_devices`, `device_json`, `event_devices`.
- **Kiosk claims already work except the method**: `device_operator` accepts `kiosk` with `scan_quests`;
  `device_claim_check` refuses a kiosk whose quest lacks `kiosk` (`DV409 not_kiosk`), and since the CHECK
  `quest_verification_known` has no `kiosk`, no quest can have it yet. `http-errors.ts` already has `notKiosk`.
- **`apps/api/src/devices/**`**: the `device` access kind (only under `/v1/device`), `guard.ts` (the
  `__Host-qs_device` cookie through `device_session`, `request.device` as `{state: "pending" …} | {state: "paired", id,
  kind, eventId, questId, activated}`, the idempotency principal `device:{id}`, `Origin` on writes, `no-store`),
  `limits.ts` (`holdBack` over keyed throttle buckets), pairing renew and activate at 10 a minute per device, a request
  renewed for at most an hour, the `stations` platform switch (`stationsOn`).
- **Web**: `/{lang}/kiosk` renders `PairedPlaceholder kind="kiosk"` and stops at "paired" ("The kiosk starts here");
  `DevicePairing` polls `GET /v1/device` every 3 s (5 after a 429) and hands its children the view. The Devices page
  (`DevicesPanel.tsx:127`) answers a kiosk's preview with `devices.kioskLater` ("Kiosks can't be paired yet").
  `proxy.ts` sets one CSP and `Permissions-Policy: camera=()` on every page.
- **App**: staff mode's Pair a device offers stations only (`operator.devices.stationsOnly`); `operator_devices` lists

  `expo-keep-awake` is in the build (T-309).
- **The station scan** (`apps/api/src/operators/scan.ts`): opens the My QR in the API, calls the door inside a
  savepoint so a refusal still lets it read who was scanned (`operator_scan_subject`), answers `{result: completed |
  already_completed | refused, reason, detail?, player}`, and moves the ranking after a completion
  (`deps.ranking.afterCompletion`). The kiosk route copies this shape.

## Changes from the fixed design (each folded into the sections below)

1. **The door never locks the operator's assignment.** The operator removal trigger deletes the assignment (row lock)
   and then updates the device; a kiosk scan holds the device `FOR SHARE`. If the scan then took the assignment `FOR
   SHARE` (as `operator_complete_quest` does), the two would cycle. The scan reads the assignment through
   `device_paused` without a lock (it sees the row as of its snapshot): a removal waits for the scan, and the scan
   either paid before the removal or is refused after it (`DV401`).
2. **The install id stays NULL.** `record_completion`'s 7th argument is the player's phone install id
   (`quest_completion.device_id`, FR-316), which drives the `shared_device` flag. A kiosk passes NULL, never its own
   `paired_device.id`: passing it would flag every player who used the kiosk as sharing one phone.
3. **The pairing operator's own code is refused** (`self_scan`), as `operator_complete_quest` refuses an operator's own
   My QR (OY409): otherwise an operator could pair a kiosk and pay themselves with no one watching.
4. **The kiosk's idempotency key is namespaced** inside the door: `kiosk:{device_id}:{key}`, with the
   `Idempotency-Key` required to be a UUID on this route (keeps it under the column's 255 characters).
   `record_completion` refuses a `kiosk:` key with any other method and the `kiosk` method with any other key (QK422),
   so no player route can collide with or replay a kiosk scan.
5. **A public screen names no one on a refusal.** The station's red card shows the player to the operator; the kiosk's
   red card shows only the reason, and every reason that would say something about the person (banned, paused, removed,
   left) reads "This quest isn't open to you now". Green and orange show the @username and the avatar (ADR-244,
   Q-208).
6. **Erasure does not remove `kiosk_scan`.** The fixed design said erasure removes it with its completion; erasure keeps
   completions (it only clears `device_id`, 0050:891). `kiosk_scan` holds no personal data (a completion id, a device
   id, tenant ids), so it stays, like the completion. The go-live wipe deletes completions, and `kiosk_scan` cascades.
7. **The kiosk's quest state needs its own reader.** `screen_quest_state` (0057) answers `method_off` for any quest
   without `screen_qr`, so `device_view` would show every kiosk as "no longer uses this screen". New internal
   `device_quest_state(quest_row, kind)` reads the kind's method; `device_view` is restated to use it.
   `screen_code_for_device` is unchanged.
8. **The bad-code pause** is "10 unreadable or forged codes in a minute, paused until that minute has passed" rather
   than "20 in 10 minutes, then a minute": the existing `holdBack` limiter answers a sliding window with its own
   Retry-After (at most 60 s), and 20 in 10 minutes could keep a kiosk paused for up to 10.
9. **`kiosk` combines with any other scan method** (printed QR, My QR, screen QR, NFC), not only "printed, screen or
   alone": it is a separate way in, and each method keeps its own proof and rules.
10. **The ranking moves after a kiosk completion**, as after a station scan (`afterCompletion`).
11. **F-274 says kiosks accept the wallet pass's fixed code**; ADR-242 makes kiosks read only the live code. The pass is
    not built; kiosks refuse its fixed code as they refuse My QR, and F-274 is amended pending **Q-207**.

## The live code (purpose 0x08; ADR-242)

- `qr.ts` gains purpose **0x08 `live`**: `createLiveCodeKey(secret)` (its own key derived from `QR_SECRET`, as every
  purpose), `liveCode(key, playerId, window)`, `verifyLiveCode(key, token)` → `{kind: "valid", playerId, window} |
  {kind: "not_questify" | "forged"}`. id = the player id, version = the 30-second window number. Stateless: nothing is
  stored per code. The token is the raw 50-character code (as My QR), never a URL, so a phone camera opens nothing.
- **`GET /v1/me/live-code`** (player bearer) → `{code, changesInMs}`. The window comes from the database
  (`SELECT * FROM screen_window()`), never the phone's clock; the API signs. 6 a minute per player (bucket
  `liveCode`); `403 feature_off` while the platform `kiosks` switch is off (read through `settings.forEvent(undefined)`,
  as `usernameChanges`). A read: no row is written; the code and the token never reach a log line.
- A My QR reset (`/v1/me/qr/reset`) does not touch live codes (they carry no `my_qr_version`); a player who signs out
  everywhere can fetch no more.

### The app's "For kiosks" tab

- `my-qr.tsx` gets two tabs: **My QR** (today's screen, unchanged: cached, works offline) and **For kiosks**.
- For kiosks fetches `GET /v1/me/live-code` on open, then again `changesInMs` plus 0 to 1 s after each answer (relative
  timers, never the phone's clock), so 2 a minute; well under 6. It shows the same QR component, the emptying bar the
  station page has (rebuilt here in React Native from `changesInMs`), and "Hold this up to the kiosk's camera. It
  changes every 30 seconds".
- **Online only**: the live code is never cached (not in SecureStore, not in memory past its window). Offline or after a
  failed fetch the tab drops the code once its window plus grace has passed and says "Kiosks need the internet.
  Staffed stations still read your My QR" with a Retry. `feature_off` says "Kiosks aren't available right now". A 429
  waits for Retry-After.
- The screen stays awake on this tab (`expo-keep-awake`, already in the build); the brightness is left alone.
- Leaving the tab or the screen stops the timer. Signing out drops it.
- Web build of the app (FR-1816): the same tab works in the browser.

## The kiosk page (`/{lang}/kiosk`)

- `PairedPlaceholder` for kiosks is replaced by a `KioskScanner` component inside `DevicePairing` (so pairing,
  activation, the 3-second state poll and `device_unpaired` handling stay shared). Paired and live, it shows: the
  quest's title and points, "Show your live code to the camera", "In Questify: My QR → For kiosks", the camera view
  (mirrored for a front camera), the label small in a corner, the event's name. Big type for a tablet at arm's length;
  RTL; Western digits.
- **States**: unpaired (the pairing code); paired, not activated; camera not yet allowed ("Allow the camera" button, as
  iOS Safari wants a gesture); camera denied ("This kiosk needs the camera…"); no camera; live; shut (`paused` "Paused by
  the event team"; `method_off` "This quest no longer uses this kiosk"; not started; ended; hidden; event ended → the
  blank Questify mark); `kiosks` switch off ("Not available"); offline ("Reconnecting…", the camera keeps running but
  nothing is sent); slowed down ("Paused for a moment", from a 429).
- **Camera**: `getUserMedia({video: {facingMode: "user"}})` (a tablet on a stand faces the player), muted, inline; a
  "Switch camera" button for a tablet mounted the other way, remembered in `localStorage` (a per-device convenience,
  wrapped in try/catch). The Screen Wake Lock as the station page. Frames are decoded on the device; **no frame,
  picture or video ever leaves the page**, only a decoded string.
- **Decoding** (ADR-245): `zxing-wasm` **3.1.5**, the `zxing-wasm/reader` subpath only, imported dynamically by the
  kiosk component alone (no other page's bundle grows). `prepareZXingModule` with `locateFile` pointing at the
  self-hosted `/zxing/zxing_reader-3.1.5.wasm`, never the library's CDN default (CSP `connect-src 'self'` would block it
  anyway). 4 frames a second: the video frame drawn to a canvas scaled to at most 640 px, `readBarcodes(imageData,
  {formats: ["QRCode"], maxNumberOfSymbols: 1})`, on the main thread (a worker only if the on-device test shows jank).
  The exact `prepareZXingModule`/`readBarcodes` names are checked against the 3.1.5 docs (context7) at build.
- **What is sent**: only a string that looks like a Questify code (43 to 60 base64url characters); anything else (a
  URL, a product barcode, junk) shows "This isn't a Questify code" locally and sends nothing. A string just sent is
  dropped for 5 seconds (one held-up phone is one scan). One scan in flight at a time; a new code read while one is in
  flight waits.
- **Sending**: `POST /v1/device/kiosk-scans {code}` with a fresh `Idempotency-Key` (`crypto.randomUUID()`) per scan; a
  network failure retries once with the same key, then shows "Reconnecting…". **Never queued**: a scan that cannot be
  sent is dropped (a queued one would be paid with a dead window anyway).
- **Result card** for 4 seconds, `role="status"` / `aria-live`: green with the avatar, @username and "+{points}
  points" (with ×2 when boosted); orange "Already done" with the avatar and @username; red with the reason only (no
  one named). Never a score, balance or rank.
- **Headers** (ADR-245): `proxy.ts` gives the exact paths `/ar/kiosk` and `/en/kiosk` (no sub-paths) `Permissions-Policy:
  camera=(self), microphone=(), geolocation=(), payment=(), usb=()` and adds `'wasm-unsafe-eval'` to `script-src`.
  Every other page keeps `camera=()` and no wasm. Robots `noindex`, `no-referrer` (as now).
- **The wasm file**: copied from `node_modules/zxing-wasm/dist/reader/zxing_reader.wasm` into
  `apps/web/public/zxing/zxing_reader-3.1.5.wasm` and committed (about 1 MiB; the version in the name so a long cache
  never serves a stale one); a unit test checks its SHA-256 equals the installed package's file, so a version bump that
  forgets the copy fails. Served with `application/wasm` (Next's static files; checked in the e2e).
- **New dependency** `zxing-wasm` 3.1.5 (MIT, published 2026-10-06; 3.1.6 of 2026-10-19 is inside the 7-day cooldown at
  the plan date and is re-checked at build). Reason: Safari has no `BarcodeDetector`; one decoder everywhere; the
  reader alone, no writer. `npm audit` at moderate before installing, pinned exactly in `apps/web/package.json`.

## The kiosk door (migration 0058)

### The method

- `quest_verification_known` widened: `verification <@ ARRAY['quest_qr', 'my_qr', 'nfc', 'screen_qr', 'kiosk']` and
  `NOT ('kiosk' = ANY (verification)) OR type = 'scan_qr'`; every other clause as 0057.
- `quest_completion_method_check` gains `kiosk`. `quest_completion_screen_idx` is replaced by
  `quest_completion_window_idx ON (enrollment_id, quest_id, created_at DESC) WHERE method IN ('screen_qr', 'kiosk')`.
- Shared registry (`packages/shared/src/quests/registry.ts`): `scan_qr.verifications` gains `kiosk`; the builder's
  checks follow the table (the table is the second wall). `member_event_quests` keeps its shape; `verification` lists
  `kiosk`.

### `kiosk_complete_quest(scanned uuid, live_window integer, request_key text)`

Definer, granted to `questify_app`, `app_device_id()` required. Returns `(completion_id, points, occurrence, replayed,
boosted)`.

1. Reads its device row `FOR SHARE` (`WHERE id = app_device_id()`); not found, not activated, not `device_alive`, or
   `kind <> 'kiosk'` → `DV401`. A Revoke (`FOR UPDATE`) or a removal trigger's update waits for a scan in flight, or
   the scan sees `revoked`: paid before or refused after, never half.
2. `device_paused(d)` → `DV403 paused` (CTO 3 of T-309; read without locking the assignment, change 1). The pairing
   operator's own player (`staff_assignment.player_id` of `paired_by_operator`) `= scanned` → `OY409 self_scan`
   (change 3).
3. `ensure_rehearsal_wiped(d.event_id)` before anything is read about the player (T-308 code review 2).
4. `record_completion(d.quest_id, scanned, 'kiosk', live_window, NULL, 'kiosk:' || d.id || ':' || request_key, NULL,
   NULL, NULL, NULL, d.event_id)`: the reserved-key check, the quest read, the enrollment `FOR NO KEY UPDATE` (QC404 for
   a player not in the event), the quest re-read after the wipe, replay, `quest_open_check` (QC403 inactive, QN403 with
   its detail), the method check, the window, the one-pay rule, every cap, the boost. Install id NULL (change 2); the
   ledger's actor is `player` (the player presented their own proof); the kiosk is named in `kiosk_scan`.
5. On a fresh completion (not `replayed`), inserts `kiosk_scan (completion_id, device_id, organizer_id, event_id)`.

`request_key` must be 36 characters (a UUID, checked again here: QK422), so the namespaced key is 79.

### `record_completion` restated (from 0057:802)

- Reserved keys: as 0057, plus `(request_key LIKE 'kiosk:%') <> (proof_method = 'kiosk')` → QK422 (change 4).
- The method check gains `(quest_row.type = 'scan_qr' AND proof_method = 'kiosk' AND 'kiosk' = ANY
  (quest_row.verification))`.
- The window: `IF proof_method IN ('screen_qr', 'kiosk') AND NOT screen_window_ok(proof_version, now())` → `QV422
  expired`. One rule for both rotating codes.
- One pay per code: `IF proof_method IN ('screen_qr', 'kiosk') AND EXISTS (… c.method = proof_method AND c.created_at >
  now() - interval '45 seconds')` → QD409, under the enrollment lock. So one held-up live code pays a repeatable quest
  once, while two kiosks of two quests each pay once.
- `kiosk` is boosted like a scan. Everything else is 0057's text. `complete_quest` is not restated: it still refuses
  `kiosk` (`wrong_method`), tested. `operator_complete_quest` unchanged (fixed My QR only).

### `kiosk_scan`

`kiosk_scan (completion_id bigint PRIMARY KEY REFERENCES quest_completion ON DELETE CASCADE, device_id uuid REFERENCES
paired_device ON DELETE SET NULL, organizer_id uuid NOT NULL, event_id uuid NOT NULL, created_at timestamptz NOT NULL
DEFAULT now())`, index on `(device_id, created_at)`. RLS forced, PUBLIC revoked, no grant; written only by
`kiosk_complete_quest`. The go-live wipe deletes rehearsal completions and their `kiosk_scan` rows cascade (no wipe
restatement); erasure keeps both (change 6). A deleted quest or event deletes the device (SET NULL here) and its
completions as today.

### Readers restated or added

- `device_quest_state(quest_row quest, device_kind text)` (internal): as `screen_quest_state`, but the method is
  `screen_qr` for a station and `kiosk` for a kiosk. `device_view()` restated to use it (change 7).
- `kiosk_scan_subject(scanned uuid)` (definer, granted, device required): the scanned player's `username` and `avatar`,
  only when they hold an enrollment in the device's event, else no row. Read after the door, inside the same
  transaction, as `operator_scan_subject`.
- `device_json(d)` restated: adds `scans` (kiosk completions through `kiosk_scan` in the last 24 hours) for a kiosk,
  NULL otherwise. `event_devices` and `operator_devices` pick it up.
- `operator_devices(target_event)` restated: adds `kioskQuests` (scan quests with `kiosk` on, inside the quest limit,
  while `scan_quests` is on), beside `quests` (stations).

## API

- **`POST /v1/device/kiosk-scans`** `{code: string (1 to 200)}`, device (paired, activated, `kind = kiosk`; any other
  kind is 401 `device_unpaired`), **Idempotency-Key** (a UUID, else 422 `invalid_request`), `Origin` checked (the
  guard) → `200 {result: "completed", player: {username, avatar}, points, boosted, replayed}` | `{result:
  "already_completed", player}` | `{result: "refused", reason}`, the station scan's shape with no `player` on a refusal.
  The module is `apps/api/src/devices/kiosk.ts`, routes in `devices/routes.ts`.
- **Opening the code** in the API: `verifyLiveCode` valid → the door. Not valid: `verifyMyCode` valid (the fixed My QR,
  or later a pass code) → `refused needs_live_code` (not counted as bad); otherwise → `refused invalid_code` (counted
  as bad, logged `kiosk.scan_refused {problem: not_questify | forged}`).
- **Refusal map** (DB code → reason on the card): QV422 `expired` → `expired`; QV422 `wrong_method` → `method_off`;
  QC404 → `not_in_event`; QC403 (any) and QN403 (any) → `not_open` (change 5: never the detail on a public screen; the
  detail goes to the log); QD409 → `already_completed`; OY409 → `self_scan` (card: "This kiosk can't scan this
  account"); QK422 → 422 `idempotency_key_reused`; DV403 `paused` → `refused paused`; DV401 → 401 `device_unpaired`
  (the page starts over).
- **Limits** (`limits.ts`): `kioskScans` 30 a minute per device (bucket `kioskScan`); `kioskBadCodes` 10 a minute per
  device (bucket `kioskBadCode`, recorded only for `invalid_code`, checked before every scan): reached, every scan
  answers 429 with Retry-After until the minute passes, and the page shows "Paused for a moment" (change 8);
  `liveCodes` 6 a minute per player. Per player at the kiosk: the quest's caps, the 45-second rule, T-209's burst flags
  (the trigger counts every non-manual completion, kiosk ones included).
- **Switch**: platform `kiosks` in `packages/shared/src/config/registry.ts` (`gated: false, level: "platform"`, on by
  default; ADR-076's paid add-on gates it later, with activation). Off: `kiosk-scans` and `live-code` answer 403
  `feature_off`, the kiosk page shows "Not available", the app's tab says so; pairing, the Devices page and Revoke stay.
- **After a completion**: `deps.ranking.afterCompletion(playerId, completionId)` (a ranking failure never fails the
  scan; change 10).
- **Logs**: `kiosk.scan_completed {questId, replayed}`, `kiosk.scan_refused {reason, detail?, problem?}`; never the
  code, the player id or the username. The redaction test gains a live code and a kiosk scan body.
- `routes.int.test.ts` classifies both routes (access kind, idempotency, limits).

## Rate limits and abuse at an unwatched kiosk (SECURITY.md gains these rows)

| Threat | Answer |
|---|---|
| A screenshot of a live code | dead 15 s after its window changed (45 s at most); `expired` |
| The fixed My QR (or a screenshot of it) | `needs_live_code`, nothing paid |
| A live code relayed live by video | pays its owner once per quest (45-s rule), within the caps; burst flags; risk accepted in ADR-242 |
| One code held up twice, or two phones showing one code | one pay (45-s rule under the enrollment lock; the page's 5-s drop) |
| The operator who paired the kiosk scanning themselves | `self_scan` (change 3) |
| Forged or junk codes to probe the signature | not Questify-shaped: dropped in the page; shaped but forged: counted, 10 a minute pause the kiosk |
| A prankster pausing the kiosk with forged codes | at most a minute per 10 codes, from in front of the camera; accepted |
| The tablet taken, or its cookie read through an unlocked browser | the cookie works elsewhere until Revoke, the event's end + 24 h or 30 idle days; it still pays only players whose live codes it is shown (the relay ceiling); the Devices page shows last seen and scans in the last 24 h; the pairing dialog's tip says to lock the tablet to the page (Guided Access, screen pinning) |
| A device cookie of another kind (a station) | `kiosk-scans` refuses it (401) |
| A kiosk of event A scanning a player of event B | `not_in_event`; the door takes no quest or event argument |
| A removed or de-abilitied operator's kiosk | `paused` at the next scan and poll; a removal revokes it (T-309) |
| A camera feed | decoded on the tablet; no frame leaves the page; nothing stored |
| Personal data on a public screen | the @username and the Questify avatar on green and orange only (public on the boards already, FR-120); never a score, balance, rank, status, ban or name |

## What the kiosk shows (privacy; ADR-244, Q-208)

The kiosk is a public screen. It shows the event's name, the quest's title and points, the label, and for 4 seconds
after a scan: on green the avatar, the @username and the points just earned; on orange the avatar and the @username;
on red only the reason. The username is a public pseudonym (SECURITY V8, FR-120) and the avatars are Questify's own
pictures, never photos, so nothing new is disclosed. Refusals that would describe the person (banned, paused, removed,
left the event) read as "This quest isn't open to you now" (change 5). Q-208 asks whether even green and orange should
name no one.

(`wrong_method`); cap reached; a retry with the same key (`replayed`, no second `kiosk_scan`); the same key on another
kiosk (a different namespaced key: pays separately only if the rules allow); a non-UUID key (QK422); a boost (×2,
`boosted`); a rehearsal account in a Scheduled event, then after go-live (the wipe first: refused, and the rehearsal
`kiosk_scan` gone); the pairing operator's own code (`self_scan`); operator lost `scan_quests` / quest left the limit
(`paused`; back on, pays); revoked, ended + 24 h, 30 idle days, not activated, a station's device id (DV401).

### Races and lock order

| Case | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Revoke while a kiosk scans | the scan holds the device `FOR SHARE`; the Revoke's `FOR UPDATE` waits: paid before, refused after. Crossed both orders. |
| Operator removed while their kiosk scans | the delete holds the assignment, its trigger's device update waits for the scan's `FOR SHARE`; the scan never locks the assignment (change 1), so no cycle. Crossed both orders. |
| Two kiosks scan one player at once | both lock the same enrollment `FOR NO KEY UPDATE`: one first; the second sees its completion (45-s rule for one quest; two quests both pay). Crossed. |
| One code sent twice (page retry) | the same key: `replayed` |
| Go-live while a kiosk scans | the device share, then `ensure_rehearsal_wiped`'s row, then the enrollment: the documented order; the wipe never touches `paired_device`. |
| The guard's `last_seen_at` update while scans run | its `NO KEY UPDATE` waits for in-flight scans' `FOR SHARE` (milliseconds), never the reverse. |
| An event deleted while its kiosk scans | rare; a deadlock is answered 503 `busy` (rules.md) and the page retries with the same key. |
| Lock order | `kiosk_complete_quest`: device `FOR SHARE` → rehearsal row → enrollment (record_completion's) → items; never the assignment. rules.md's lock-order paragraph gains this line. |

## Proposed decisions and questions

- **ADR-244** What a kiosk shows and refuses: the @username and avatar on green and orange only, a red card names no
  one and never a personal reason (banned, paused, removed, left → "isn't open to you now"); the pairing operator's own
  live code is refused; the kiosk's keys are `kiosk:{device}:{uuid}`, reserved for the kiosk door; the install id is
  never set from a kiosk; a kiosk never queues a scan; the ledger's actor is the player and `kiosk_scan` names the
  kiosk; the door never locks the operator's assignment.
- **ADR-245** The kiosk page: `zxing-wasm` 3.1.5 reader, its wasm committed under `public/zxing/` with a hash test
  against the installed package, decoded on the main thread at 4 frames a second; `camera=(self)` and
  `'wasm-unsafe-eval'` on `/{lang}/kiosk` only; the front camera by default with a switch; 30 scans a minute per kiosk
  and 10 bad codes a minute pause it until the minute passes; the platform `kiosks` switch (on by default).
- **Q-207** F-274 says kiosks accept the wallet pass's fixed code, and ADR-242 makes kiosks read only the live code.
  Recommended: kiosks refuse the pass's fixed code as they refuse My QR (stations and the prize desk still accept it),
  and F-274 is amended when the pass is built. Or should kiosks accept the pass with ADR-091's risk?
- **Q-208** A kiosk is a public screen: show the @username and avatar on green and orange (recommended; both are public
  on the boards), or only the colour and the points, so a bystander learns nothing?
