# T-404 — Gems and the Questify gem shop (no money, no trading)

**Plan, awaiting the independent CTO plan review (core).** Delivers FR-724, FR-725, FR-726 and the shop half of FR-415
(no price in XP). Depends on T-401 (XP, `level_reached`), T-402 (items, locker, `player_item`), T-403/T-403b
(`achievement_earned`, `questify_award`, `questify_sync`), all built. Never push, deploy, run EAS or run local mutation
testing. Choices are **Proposed ADR-284 to ADR-289 / Q-249 to Q-254**. `docs/STORE_COMPLIANCE.md` is the gate (below).

## Split (ADR-284, Q-249)

The T-403 precedent: risky ledger work and display work go to different workers, models and review levels.

| Part | Bar | Delivers | Migration |
|---|---|---|---|
| **T-404** | **CORE** (a spendable ledger, a spend door, the sync evaluator) | Gem ledger and balance; gems paid by `questify_sync` for level-ups, achievement tiers and Questify quests; the gem shop catalog, read and purchase doors; quest `gems` on the console doors; API routes and shared schemas | 0067 |
| **T-404b** | **STANDARD** (display and copy) | App: gem count on Profile, the gem shop screen with a confirmation sheet, "+N gems" in the sync toasts, gems on the Questify quests list; console: the quest form's Gems field; the two new frame designs in `frameDesign`; every string | none |

T-404 is checked when its API and DB suites pass and its code review is GO. "Done when" (gems cannot be bought or traded
and are earned only from Questify activity) is proven by T-404's DB and route tests; T-404b proves on screen that no
purchase or gift option exists (FR-725's two Givens). T-404b starts after the console publish-dialog review of f68ad34
is closed (it edits `ConsoleQuestify.tsx`).

**Out of scope (SCOPE.md and existing tasks):** buying gems or items, trading, gifting, converting event points to
gems (FEATURES "Won't"); gems from the daily streak or daily challenge (FR-421, T-405/T-406 must not write gems); console
editing of the shop catalog, prices and gem amounts (FR-1403, T-709 note); the Shop tab in the bottom bar (FR-830, T-408;
until then the shop opens from Profile and the locker); character items in the shop (no art yet, Q-253); a gem history
screen (not required by any FR; T-708 exports the ledger). No new service or dependency. No random rewards.

## Store compliance gate (ADR-289, Q-254)

Checked against `STORE_COMPLIANCE.md` 1.6 (Apple 3.1.1, 3.1.1(a), 3.1.3(f)), 1.12/2.9 (contests, gambling), 1.14 (age
rating) and PD-05/PD-22:

- Gems are **earned only** (closed source list in SQL); nothing in the app is sold, so no IAP applies and the "purchased
  currency may not expire" rule does not arise. Gems never expire anyway.
- **No purchase UI**: no "add gems", "get more" or price-in-money control, no link out. When a player is short, the shop
  says how gems are earned (level-ups, achievements, Questify quests) and nothing else.
- **No trading or gifting**: no door moves gems or items between players; the ledger has no transfer source and the
  item provenance has no gift source; another player's gems are never shown (not on the public profile, rankings or
  locker).
- **No chance**: every item has a fixed price and is chosen by the player; no mystery box, no random grant. Age rating
  unchanged (no gambling descriptor).
- **No cash value**: gems buy cosmetics only (frames, titles, later characters) that give no gameplay, points or prize
  advantage; event rewards stay priced in event points only; nothing is priced in XP (FR-415).
- Wording: the action is "Get" (احصل عليه), not "Buy"; the shop shows "Gems are earned by playing. They can't be bought."
  STORE_COMPLIANCE 1.6's scenario table gains the row "Gems earned by play, spent on cosmetic items in the Questify shop |
  No | nothing is sold; no buy button; no trading" (written at build).

## T-404 (CORE) — migration 0067

All new tables: ENABLE and FORCE RLS, REVOKE ALL FROM PUBLIC and `questify_app`; definers `SET search_path = public,
pg_temp`, PUBLIC EXECUTE revoked in the same statement group; only the doors named here get `questify_app` EXECUTE.
Every player door rechecks an active caller through `app_player_id()`. Deployed migrations untouched; 0063 to 0066 are
undeployed but stay untouched too (0067 replaces what it changes). Every table lock is taken up front under
`lock_timeout = '10s'` in the order a scan takes them (`player_xp`, then `player_item`), the 0066 precedent.

### The ledger (ADR-285, Q-250)

| Object | Shape | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `player_xp.gems` | `bigint NOT NULL DEFAULT 0 CHECK (gems >= 0)` | The balance, a cache of `gem_entry` like `xp`. On the row every sync and every purchase already locks FOR UPDATE, so gems add no lock and no new lock order. The CHECK makes an overspend impossible even if a door is wrong |
| `gem_entry` | `id bigint identity`, `player_id → player_xp`, `delta ≠ 0`, `source` level/achievement/questify/purchase, `source_level`, `achievement_id`, `tier`, `questify_award_id`, `purchase_id`, `created_at` | Shape CHECK: exactly the key of its source is set; credits `delta > 0`, a purchase `delta < 0`. Composite FKs bind each key to the same player: (player_id, source_level) → `level_reached`, (player_id, achievement_id, tier) → `achievement_earned`, (questify_award_id, player_id) → `questify_award (id, player_id)` (new UNIQUE), (purchase_id, player_id) → `gem_purchase (id, player_id)`. UNIQUE (player_id, source_level), (player_id, achievement_id, tier), (questify_award_id), (purchase_id): one entry per source row ever. Append-only for every role (UPDATE/DELETE/TRUNCATE refused, ENABLE ALWAYS); kept on the erasure tombstone (no personal data), as `xp_entry` |

There is no transfer, gift, adjustment or expiry source. FR-421 and FR-1106 become structural: a later task can only
add a source through a migration that a review sees. Invariant: `player_xp.gems = sum(gem_entry.delta)` for every player
(asserted after every DB test file's random sequence).

### Earning (ADR-286, Q-251)

**One writer.** `questify_sync()` (replaced in 0067) is the only door that credits gems. After the tiers and the Questify
quest awards, under the same locks (player FOR KEY SHARE → `player_xp` FOR UPDATE), it pays every source row of the
caller that has no `gem_entry` yet:

- each `level_reached` row (level ≥ 2): `levelUp` gems;
- each `achievement_earned` row: the tier's gems (`bronze`, `silver`, `gold`);
- each `questify_award` row whose quest has `gems > 0`: the quest's gems.

Inserts use `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING RETURNING`, then one `UPDATE player_xp SET gems = gems + paid`. Nothing is added to
the completion transaction (scan latency and lock set unchanged, ADR-274): a level reached by a scan is paid at the next
sync, about 15 s later (ADR-281's debounce); a level reached by the sync's own quest XP is paid in the same call. The
unlocked pre-check gains `gems_due(who)` (any unpaid source row), so a sync with nothing due still takes no lock.

**Retroactive.** Rows earned before T-404 (levels since T-401) are paid at the first sync after the deploy, so a veteran
is not behind a newcomer. At most 29 levels and 15 tiers per player, a bounded one-time payment.

**Amounts are data.** `platform_setting` key `gems` `{levelUp: 20, bronze: 10, silver: 25, gold: 50}`, each CHECKed
1–1000 on write (0039's `xp` pattern), read by `gem_amounts()` with those fallbacks; an amount change applies to rows
paid later. `questify_quest.gems integer NOT NULL DEFAULT 0 CHECK (0–1000)`; the two seeded quests get 25. A quest's
`gems` is frozen once the quest can pay (with metric, target and campaign, ADR-280), so raising it later can never pay
earlier awardees after the fact. `questify_quest_save`, `questify_quest_json` and `own_questify_quests` (replaced) carry
`gems`; console editing of `gems` setting values and level/tier amounts waits for T-709.

Sync answer gains `gems: {earned, balance}` (`earned` 0 when nothing was paid; the early return answers the balance
read without a lock).

### The gem shop (ADR-287, ADR-288, Q-252, Q-253)

| Object | Shape | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `gem_shop_item` | `item_id text PK → item`, `price integer CHECK 1–100000`, `status` active/retired, `position smallint`, `created_at` | The catalog. Seeded only (T-709 edits it). Retired items stay owned and equippable; they are no longer offered |
| `gem_purchase` | `id bigint identity`, `player_id → player_xp`, `item_id → gem_shop_item`, `price` (what was paid), `request_key uuid`, `created_at`; UNIQUE (player_id, item_id), (player_id, request_key), (id, player_id) | One copy per player. Append-only, kept on the tombstone (no personal data) |
| `player_item` (altered) | `source` gains `shop`; `source_purchase_id bigint` | Source CHECK rewritten: level and souvenir as 0064, `shop` → only `source_purchase_id` set; composite FK (player_id, item_id, source_purchase_id) → `gem_purchase (player_id, item_id, id)`. `owned_item_guard` (replaced) adds the new column to the frozen set |
| `item` (altered) | frame visual key allowlist `frame-(0[1-9]|1[0-9]|20)` | Six new shipped procedural frame designs for the shop (T-404b renders them); souvenirs may use them too |

**Exclusive sources.** An item is sold only if it is no level reward and no souvenir: `souvenir_catalog_guard` (replaced)
also guards `gem_shop_item` and refuses an item that `level_item` or `souvenir_rule` names, and the reverse. Without this
a bought item could collide with a later `grant_level_item` on `player_item`'s (player, item) key and abort a scan.

**`gem_shop()`** (definer, caller-bound): `{gems, items: [{id, kind, name {ar,en}, visualKey, price, owned}]}`, active
items by position; `owned` reads `player_item` (any source). Never another player's data.

**`gem_buy(item, seen_price, request_key)`** (definer, caller-bound) — lock order: player FOR KEY SHARE (active, else
`PI401`) → `player_xp` FOR UPDATE → then, in this order:
1. Replay: a `gem_purchase` with this `request_key` → the original answer (`replayed: true`); same key for another item
   → `GS422` `key_reused`. Checked after the lock, so two concurrent identical requests serialize and the second replays.
2. Item not found or retired → `GS404`.
3. Already owned (any source) → `GS409 owned`.
4. `seen_price` ≠ current price → `GS409 price_changed` (DETAIL carries nothing secret; the app re-reads).
5. `gems < price` → `GS409 too_few_gems`; nothing written, the balance unchanged.
6. Insert `gem_purchase`, `gem_entry (purchase, −price)`, `player_item (shop)`; `UPDATE player_xp SET gems = gems − price`.
   Answer `{itemId, gems (new balance), item}`. XP is never read or written (FR-415).

Erasure: `erase_player` already deletes equipment and `player_item` under `app.erasure`; `gem_purchase` and
`gem_entry` stay on the tombstone like `xp_entry` (ADR-175's list gains both). A tombstone can neither sync nor buy.

**Seeded catalog (ADR-288, Q-253):** six frames on the new keys `frame-15`…`frame-20` and six titles, adventure-themed
names in both languages (reviewed with `ux-araby` at build), prices: titles 40–80, frames 80–150 gems. No characters
until new character art exists (FR-726 is partial for characters; recorded in SRS). The economy check: one event of
about 15 quests (1,500 XP) reaches level 5 (4 level-ups, 80 gems) plus first bronze tiers (about 20), so a first item is
reachable after one event and the whole catalog takes a season.

### API (`apps/api/src/questify/`; the event rewards shop keeps `/v1/events/{id}/shop`)

| Route | Access | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| `GET /v1/me` | player | gains `gems` (number) |
| `POST /v1/me/questify/sync` | player | gains `gems: {earned, balance}` |
| `GET /v1/me/questify-quests` | player | each quest gains `gems` |
| `GET /v1/me/gem-shop` | player | `{gems, items: [...]}` |
| `POST /v1/me/gem-shop/purchases` | player, Idempotency-Key (route replay store and the DB `request_key`) | `200 {itemId, gems, item}`; `404 gem_item_not_found`; `409 gem_shop_conflict {reason: owned|price_changed|too_few_gems, gems}`; `422 idempotency_key_reused`. Body `{itemId, price}` (zod, `price` an integer). Rate limit 10 a minute per player (existing buckets) |
| `/v1/admin/questify/quests` (save, list) | admin | quest body and view gain `gems` (0–1000); `422 invalid_questify {field: gems}`; a change once the quest can pay `409 {reason: frozen}` |

Staff sessions cannot enter player doors (T-402 pattern). No route takes a player id, so no door can name another
player. Shared zod schemas in `packages/shared/src/questify/gems.ts` (shop, purchase, refusal reasons, sync `gems`).

## T-404b (STANDARD) — surfaces

- **App:** Profile shows the gem count (the shipped `currency/gem.png`) and opens the gem shop (`app/gem-shop.tsx`);
  the locker links there too. The shop lists items as cards (frame preview on the player's own avatar, title preview on
  a name plate), price, "Owned" state; tapping "Get" opens a confirmation sheet ("Get Desert Ring for 120 gems?") whose
  confirm sends the purchase with a fresh Idempotency-Key kept for retries; success offers "Equip now" (the existing
  equip call). `too_few_gems` shows how gems are earned; `price_changed` re-reads and asks again; `owned` refreshes. The
  sync toasts add "+N gems" once per sync. Questify quests show "+25 gems" beside XP when `gems > 0`.
- **Console:** the quest form gains Gems (0–1000), read-only once frozen; the list shows it.
- **Shared:** `frameDesign` accepts `frame-15`…`frame-20` (new colours from the theme's gem and royal tokens, checked
  at 3:1 on the page).
- **Strings:** every label in `messages.ts` in Arabic and English; gem counts follow CONFIGURABILITY's plural rules
  (Arabic جوهرة/جوهرتان/جواهر by count); RTL checked; Western digits (ADR-018); no Hermes-missing built-ins.
- **Phone check** owed when a build exists: RTL, Reduce Motion, the confirmation sheet, VoiceOver/TalkBack labels.

## Tests (test first)

**T-404 DB** (`apps/api/test/db/gems.int.test.ts`, `gem-shop.int.test.ts`):
- Earning: a level-up pays `levelUp` once at the next sync; a two-level jump pays two entries; replay pays nothing; two
  concurrent syncs on two connections pay once and only one reports it; a tier pays its amount, a three-tier jump pays
  three; a quest with gems pays once, a quest with 0 pays nothing and writes no entry; pre-T-404 level rows are paid
  retroactively; amounts come from the setting and its fallbacks; the setting CHECK refuses 0, 1001 and non-integers;
  a quest's gems are frozen once it can pay.
- FR-724 negatives: gems never change event balance, score or XP; a removal (FR-1602) that drops a level takes no gems
  back (the level row stays); no other function writes `gem_entry` (a catalog query asserts the only writers are
  `questify_sync` and `gem_buy`).
- Purchase: success deducts, writes one entry, one purchase and one `shop` item, XP unchanged (FR-415); too few gems
  refused with the balance unchanged (FR-726); owned, retired, unknown and price-changed refused; replay answers the
  original; a key reused for another item refused; two concurrent purchases that only one balance covers: one succeeds,
  the other `too_few_gems`; two concurrent purchases of the same item: one item, one charge; a bought frame equips.
- Concurrency: purchase crossing a sync, a scan of the same player and `erase_player`, both orders (no deadlock, nothing
  written after erasure commits); a tombstone cannot sync or buy.
- Integrity: the ledger invariant; UPDATE/DELETE/TRUNCATE refused on `gem_entry` and `gem_purchase` for the owner role;
  the exclusivity guard both ways (`level_item`/`souvenir_rule`/`gem_shop_item`); the shape CHECK rejects every mixed
  source; a composite FK refuses another player's source row.
- Trading: no function takes a recipient; the public profile, rankings and locker answers carry no gem field (exact
  allowlist tests already pin those DTOs; they are re-run).

**Security** (`apps/api/test/security/*`): the outsider EXECUTE probe gains every new function; the cross-tenant suite
lists the new tables (an organizer reads no gem entry, purchase or shop row); the rehearsal guard list and the community
gate map gain the new tables. **Route/unit:** shapes, auth, the Idempotency-Key requirement, rate limit, refusal mapping
by SQLSTATE and DETAIL, staff refused on player doors, the shared schemas (an integer price, the reason enum).
**T-404b:** app unit tests for the shop states (owned, short, price changed, success, replay), the confirmation sheet, the
toast line, RTL snapshots; the console spec for the Gems field in both languages at phone width; `npx expo export`.

## Proposed decisions and questions

- **ADR-284 / Q-249** the split: T-404 core (DB, API, shared), T-404b standard (app, console field, strings).
- **ADR-285 / Q-250** an append-only `gem_entry` with a closed source list (level, achievement, questify, purchase), the
  balance on `player_xp` with a CHECK ≥ 0; no transfer, gift, expiry or adjustment; gems are never shown to others.
- **ADR-286 / Q-251** gems paid only by `questify_sync`, once per source row, retroactively, never in the completion
  transaction; amounts as data (setting `gems` 20/10/25/50, quest `gems` 0–1000 frozen once it can pay, seeded quests 25).
- **ADR-287 / Q-252** the gem shop: seeded catalog of exclusive items, one copy per player, a purchase door that checks
  the seen price and replays by key; catalog editing waits for T-709.
- **ADR-288 / Q-253** seeded catalog: six frames on new procedural keys and six titles, prices 40–150; no characters
  until art exists.
- **ADR-289 / Q-254** store wording and the compliance row: "Get", not "Buy"; no add-gems control; a line saying gems
  are earned and cannot be bought.
