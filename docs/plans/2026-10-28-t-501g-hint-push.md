file, undeployed; CranL runs through 0056). Where this note fills in what the plan left open: **ADR-357 / Q-326
(Proposed)**. Never push, deploy or run Stryker locally.

## How it works

- **The button.** A hidden category's card on the quest builder gets "Send a hint" (owners and editors within their event
  limit, never on an ended event). It asks `GET …/categories/{id}/hint` for the count first and confirms with "N of your
  players will be told", then `POST …/categories/{id}/hint` with an Idempotency-Key. The key is made when the dialog opens
  and reused on a resend, so a double click or a retry sends nothing twice.
- **The door** `send_category_hint(event, category, press, request)` (definer, granted to the API role): the caller must
  edit the event (`award_event_of`, as Send poll), the event live (after the go-live wipe) or in an open rehearsal (PE409
  otherwise), the category of this event and hidden (QH422), no hint of this category still being delivered (PH409), and at
  least one member left to tell (the count is above 0; PT409 `hint_nobody`, after the replay check). It
  writes **one notice through `notice_create`** (kind `category_hint`, `refs` the category id, `params` the event's name,
  marker `hint:{category}:{sha256 of the press key}`) and one audit line `category.hint_send`. A replayed press finds
  its notice and answers `replayed`. A missing kind row writes nothing and the door still commits (`sent` false).
- **The reveal is the fan-out's job, not the producer's.** `push_fan_out_batch` (restated) gets one data-modifying CTE:
  for a `category_hint` notice, each member who passes the batch's checks gets a `category_reveal` row in the same
  statement that writes their center row. Recipient and reveal are therefore the same set, by construction, and the
  producer still calls only `notice_create`. The reveal does not depend on a device, a permission, a mute, the platform
  switch or a delivery: the center row and the reveal are written even for a player who refused push or muted
  `announcements`.
- **Who is a recipient** (`push_hint_ok`, inside `notice_audience_ok`, checked at the fan-out and again at the claim):
  an active enrollment of an active player (the membership rule), real members on a live event and rehearsal accounts
  on a draft or scheduled one (the status rule), not an operator the owner keeps from playing; **joined at or before the
  press** (`enrollment.joined_at <= notice.created_at`; a player who joins, or re-joins, after the press is not told);
  the category still hidden and the member in its audience; and **not told by an earlier hint of this category** since
  they joined (a second press reaches only members not yet told). `notice_visible` waives `sees_category` for this one
  kind (the hint is what grants it) and keeps every other check (the category belongs to the event).
- **The text** names only the event: "Something new is hidden in {event}." / «في {event} شيء جديد مخفي.», nameless
  "Something new is hidden in this event." / «في الفعالية شيء جديد مخفي.». Never the category's name (it is the
  secret). Topic `announcements` (mutable), 2 hours; route: the quests tab.
- **The count** (`category_hint_preview`, STABLE) is one set-based statement over the same rules, so it stays under the
  API's 5 s limit at 50,000 members; a database test requires it to equal the fan-out's recipients on a crowd that holds
  every excluded kind and on each audience rule.
- **Cost at 50,000 members** (by hand, `test/perf/push-hint-timing.int`; the review run of 2026-10-28, ADR-357 (6)): the
  count 305 to 366 ms with or without one exclusion entry (the exclusion rule is an inline `NOT EXISTS`; asked through
  `excluded_from_event` per row it took 2.4 s as soon as the event had one entry) and 228 to 241 ms with a tag audience of
  10,000 guests (the first version took 12.6 s because `in_audience` ran per row), the press 311 to 327 ms (it counts
  first), the fan-out 41.2 s in 102 calls of 500 (median 409 ms, slowest 474 ms; 1,000 a call took 825 ms median).
  The generic event fan-out is unchanged (a live poll 31.0 s, an opening 27.3 s). The hard gates hold; 40 s is over the 30 s
  soft target.
- **The dashboard** keeps its hint logic in `useCategoryHint` (the count, the confirmation, the key made when the dialog
  opens) so `QuestBuilder` does not grow past its function-limit baseline; the card's buttons became `CategoryActions`.

## Care points and where they are tested (`test/db/push-hint.int.test.ts` unless noted)

| Care point | Test |
|---|---|
| Reveal limited to exactly the recipients | a crowd (real member, left, banned, pending, rehearsal account, operator kept from playing, banned player, erased, outside the audience, joined after the press): only the real member gets a reveal and a center row; the count equals the recipients |
| Muted or undelivered push does not hide it; push refused still sees it | a recipient with `announcements` muted, one with no device, one whose send fails: all revealed with a center row; the quest list shows the category |
| Once per press | the same key twice: one notice, `replayed`; a second key while the first is delivered: PH409; after it, a new press reaches only members not yet told |
| Nobody to tell | a press with no member, and three new-key presses after everyone was told: PT409 `hint_nobody`, no notice; the key that sent the hint still answers `replayed` |
| Told again after a re-join | a told member who leaves and re-joins is in the next count and the next press's recipients (`told.created_at >= joined_at`, in `push_hint_ok` and in the count) |
| A stuck notice does not block | a notice older than 10 minutes whose fan-out never finished does not make `pending` true or refuse a press |
| Go-live wipe | a press on a live event with a rehearsal account left behind wipes it (`live_wiped_at` set, the account gone) and tells the real members only |
| Roles | owner and editor press; an editor outside the event limit, another organizer's owner, a player and the public key get AN404 or permission denied |
| Deny by default | outsider and cross-tenant suites list every new function (no PUBLIC grant, only the two doors granted); `category_reveal` still has no grant |
| A broken kind cannot roll back the door | with the kind row missing the door answers `sent` false, writes no notice, no reveal, no audit line |
| Every kind row has a template and a route | `push-store.int` (existing) |
| Joined after | a player who joins between the press and the fan-out is not revealed and not told; scanning the secret code still reveals |
